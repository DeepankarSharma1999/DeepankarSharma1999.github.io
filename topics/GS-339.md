## GS-339: Indo-Pacific, QUAD and ASEAN

Mains GS2 + Prelims GS1 · International Relations · India and world

*Goal:* Compare the Indo-Pacific idea, Quad cooperation and ASEAN-led institutions.

### A region and two different forms of cooperation

The Indo-Pacific connects the Indian and Pacific Oceans through trade, security and ecological relationships. The Quad brings together India, Australia, Japan and the United States for practical cooperation. ASEAN is a Southeast Asian regional organization with its own institutions. A geographic concept, a four-country grouping and a regional organization should not be used interchangeably.

### Explain ASEAN centrality

ASEAN centrality supports a meaningful role for ASEAN-led regional processes rather than treating Southeast Asian states as passive objects of major-power competition. Maritime awareness, disaster response, infrastructure and technology can offer areas for cooperation. A free and open region requires predictable rules and respect for sovereignty, while disagreements over interpretations and capabilities still need diplomacy.
