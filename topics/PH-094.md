## PH-094: Poynting theorem and energy flow

Physics Paper I · Physics · EM waves and radiation

*Goal:* Understand Poynting theorem and energy flow, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Poynting theorem and energy flow belongs to EM waves and radiation. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Start from Maxwell’s equations and the material relations. For a plane wave verify E, B and propagation direction, then use the Poynting vector for energy flow. At an interface apply tangential boundary conditions; for radiation laws state the variable and integration range.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive Poynting theorem from Maxwell equations and interpret each term. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: For E=100 V/m plane wave in vacuum, express time-average intensity in terms of ε0 and c.
