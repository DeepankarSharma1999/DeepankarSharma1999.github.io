## GS-018: Mughal expansion and key rulers

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Medieval India

*Goal:* Explain Mughal expansion and key rulers and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Mughal expansion combined military conquest, alliances and administrative integration. Akbar's consolidation differed from later rulers' circumstances; an emperor's formal claim exceeded effective control in some regions.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Akbar combined conquest with alliances and a graded service nobility; later emperors inherited a larger but costlier empire. Mughal sovereignty was negotiated through provincial officials, zamindars and local elites rather than exercised uniformly from the court. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Separate an emperor's formal claim from effective control on the ground.
