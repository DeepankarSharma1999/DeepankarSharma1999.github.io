Prelims CSAT (qualifying) · CSAT · Quantitative aptitude

*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Speed, time and distance and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Speed, time and distance is a quantitative aptitude skill. Translate words into variables, write the relation before calculating, and estimate the answer range. Keep fractions until the last step when possible. If options are available, test them only after understanding the relation; record whether an error came from concept, setup, arithmetic or time pressure.
