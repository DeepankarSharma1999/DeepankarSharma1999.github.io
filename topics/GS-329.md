## GS-329: India–China ties and border issues

Mains GS2 + Prelims GS1 · International Relations · India and world

*Goal:* Separate the India–China boundary dispute from wider economic and strategic ties.

### Three linked dimensions

India–China relations combine an unresolved boundary, substantial economic exchange and competition over regional influence. The Line of Actual Control is not a mutually settled international boundary, and perceptions of its alignment differ in places. Distinguish a sovereignty claim, a patrolling arrangement and a final boundary settlement: agreement on one does not automatically resolve the others.

### Manage competition and interdependence

Border infrastructure, diplomatic dialogue and military communication address different risks. Trade links can create benefits and dependence at the same time, especially where critical inputs have few substitutes. Agreements in 1993 and 1996 sought to maintain peace and confidence along the LAC. Assess their purpose separately from later implementation disputes or claims about the latest ground situation.
