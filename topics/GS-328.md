## GS-328: Social justice legal mechanisms and commissions

Mains GS2 · Social Justice · Rights, welfare and services

*Goal:* Choose the right institution and remedy for a social-justice failure.

### Protection uses several legal routes

Constitutional equality, anti-discrimination laws, protective legislation and welfare duties form a wider framework. Courts can review unlawful action, while commissions investigate or advise within their mandates. A constitutional commission and a statutory commission have different legal origins. Neither label alone tells you that every recommendation is binding or that the body can impose every requested remedy.

### Match the complaint to the power

Ask what right is affected, which authority acted and what relief is needed. Administrative correction, compensation, investigation and judicial enforcement are different remedies. Accessible legal aid, language support and protection from retaliation help turn formal rights into usable rights. Track implementation after an order or recommendation; institutional activity without follow-through can leave the original exclusion unchanged.
