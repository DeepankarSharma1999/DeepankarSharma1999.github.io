## GS-371: Attitude-behaviour relationship

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Attitude, aptitude and emotional intelligence

*Goal:* Explain Attitude-behaviour relationship and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Attitudes influence behaviour but do not determine it mechanically. Social norms, perceived control, incentives and situational pressures affect whether a person acts consistently with a belief. Observing one action therefore provides limited evidence about an enduring attitude.

### How to apply it

The relationship can also run from behaviour to attitude through experience and attempts to reduce inconsistency. A carefully designed practice can build confidence and change expectations. For public policy, distinguish expressed support from actual ability to comply: a household may support waste segregation but lack separate collection. Effective interventions align information, accessible facilities and credible norms rather than blaming attitudes for every implementation failure.
