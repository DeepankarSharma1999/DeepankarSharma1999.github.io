## CT-034: Decision making and problem solving

Prelims CSAT (qualifying) · CSAT · Reasoning / decision making

*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Decision making and problem solving and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Decision making and problem solving is a reasoning / decision making skill. List only the facts that are given, symbolize the constraints, and draw a small table, diagram or Venn set. A valid conclusion must follow in every arrangement allowed by the statements. For decision questions, identify stakeholders, legality, urgency and the least harmful workable action.

### Timed application

Attempt this before viewing the hint: A rural clinic has one ambulance and two simultaneous emergencies, one immediately life-threatening. What principle should guide first allocation? Give yourself two minutes, choose an answer and write one line explaining why. Then compare with: Suggested answer / check: Urgency/severity-based triage under transparent criteria, while coordinating backup. Retry a similar item after three study days and explain why every rejected option fails.
