## GS-061: Economic liberalisation and post-1991 transformations

Mains GS1 · History · Post-1947 India

*Goal:* Explain Economic liberalisation and post-1991 transformations and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

The 1991 reforms responded to macroeconomic crisis through stabilization and structural changes. Liberalization altered licensing, trade and investment rules, with uneven effects across sectors and social groups.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. The 1991 response combined short-term stabilization with changes to industrial licensing, trade, finance and foreign investment. Reform increased competition and integration while its benefits, costs and employment effects varied across sectors and groups. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Liberalisation, privatisation and globalisation overlap but are not interchangeable terms or one single policy.

### Worked example

Abolishing many industrial licences changed entry conditions; it did not abolish all regulation.
