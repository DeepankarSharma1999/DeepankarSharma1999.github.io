## GS-009: Gupta age, science and literature

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Ancient India

*Goal:* Explain Gupta age, science and literature and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Gupta-era evidence includes inscriptions, coins and Sanskrit literature. Advances associated with this wider period include Aryabhata's mathematical astronomy; prosperity and social conditions varied by region.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Build the topic around three defensible links. Gupta history is reconstructed through inscriptions, coins, literary works and archaeology rather than one continuous chronicle. The period saw influential Sanskrit court culture and advances in mathematics and astronomy, including Aryabhata's work. Regional prosperity and social experience varied, so a universal golden-age label conceals uneven evidence. For objective questions, classify each statement by process, chronology or scope before eliminating it. For a written answer or worked solution, state the governing idea, apply it to a concrete example, and finish with the limitation: A cultural achievement associated with the wider Gupta age need not have been created by the Gupta court.
