## GS-359: Cybercrime and national cybersecurity

Mains GS3 + Prelims GS1 · Security · Internal security

*Goal:* Explain Cybercrime and national cybersecurity and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Cybercrime uses digital systems as a target or a tool. Confidentiality protects information from unauthorized access, integrity protects it from unauthorized alteration, and availability keeps systems usable. These objectives require different controls and may conflict with cost or convenience.

### How to apply it

National cybersecurity combines prevention, detection, response and recovery across public and private systems. Backups support recovery only if tested and protected; access controls must match roles; staff awareness reduces some social-engineering risks. Attribution can be difficult because compromised infrastructure may hide an attacker's location. An effective policy considers critical infrastructure, reporting, institutional coordination and protection of personal data.

### Worked example

A hospital with encrypted offline backups can restore services after an incident more reliably than one with untested connected copies.

### Recall

- - **q:** Which security objective is harmed when a service becomes inaccessible?
  - **a:** Availability; the system may fail users even if no information was stolen.

### Common trap

Assuming installing one security product guarantees complete protection.
