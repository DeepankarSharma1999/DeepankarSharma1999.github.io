## GS-391: Use of public funds and corruption prevention

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Thinkers, administration and probity

*Goal:* Explain Use of public funds and corruption prevention and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Public funds are held for authorized public purposes, making stewardship a central ethical duty. Corruption diverts decisions or resources through improper private advantage; waste can arise even without corruption. Economy, efficiency and effectiveness examine different aspects of spending.

### How to apply it

Economy concerns resource cost, efficiency the relation between inputs and outputs, and effectiveness whether intended outcomes are achieved. Procurement safeguards, audit trails, competition and conflict management protect integrity, but controls must be usable. A low purchase price can conceal poor quality or high maintenance costs. Evaluate whole-life value and verified service outcomes while separating an honest implementation failure from deliberate misuse requiring investigation.
