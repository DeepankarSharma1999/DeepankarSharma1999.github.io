## PH-092: Displacement current and Maxwell equations

Physics Paper I · Physics · EM waves and radiation

*Goal:* Understand Displacement current and Maxwell equations, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Displacement current and Maxwell equations belongs to EM waves and radiation. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Start from Maxwell’s equations and the material relations. For a plane wave verify E, B and propagation direction, then use the Poynting vector for energy flow. At an interface apply tangential boundary conditions; for radiation laws state the variable and integration range.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive Ampère–Maxwell law and explain why displacement current is essential for charge conservation. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: For a parallel plate capacitor with dE/dt constant, obtain displacement current density in vacuum.

### Worked example

Worked route: identify the requested result in “Derive Ampère–Maxwell law and explain why displacement current is essential for charge conservation.”; select the governing principle; state assumptions; carry the algebra without skipping vector directions or boundary conditions; then interpret the result physically.
