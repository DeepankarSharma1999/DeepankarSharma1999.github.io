## PH-089: Series and parallel resonance

Physics Paper I · Physics · Current electricity

*Goal:* Understand Series and parallel resonance, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Series and parallel resonance belongs to Current electricity. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Label currents and polarities before writing Kirchhoff equations. For transients find initial and final conditions plus the time constant; for sinusoidal steady state use complex impedance and report measurable RMS values. Resonance and bandwidth must use the same circuit convention.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive the resonance condition of a series RLC circuit and its impedance at resonance. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: For L=0.1 H and C=100 μF, find resonant angular frequency.
