## GS-060: Regional aspirations and insurgencies

Mains GS1 · History · Post-1947 India

*Goal:* Explain Regional aspirations and insurgencies and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Regional aspirations may seek recognition, autonomy or statehood; insurgency involves organized armed challenge. Institutions, development and political accommodation interact with security responses.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Regional claims range from cultural recognition and statehood to autonomy, secession and armed insurgency. Punjab, the Northeast, Kashmir and other regions followed different histories of negotiation, elections, accords and coercion. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Do not equate every regional demand with separatism or assume security action alone resolves its political causes.

### Worked example

A demand for statehood within India is analytically different from secession.
