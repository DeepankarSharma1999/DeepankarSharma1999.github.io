## GS-059: Political change and Emergency 1975–77

Mains GS1 · History · Post-1947 India

*Goal:* Explain Political change and Emergency 1975–77 and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

The 1975–77 Emergency involved exceptional restrictions on civil liberties and concentrated executive power. The 1977 election demonstrated the importance of electoral accountability after emergency rule.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Emergency rule followed political conflict and a court judgment but operated through constitutional proclamation and extensive coercive measures. Press censorship, preventive detention and curtailed opposition were followed by the 1977 election and a peaceful transfer of power. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: A constitutional form of proclamation does not by itself prove that rights and institutions operated normally.

### Worked example

Distinguish a constitutional emergency provision from the political uses made of it.
