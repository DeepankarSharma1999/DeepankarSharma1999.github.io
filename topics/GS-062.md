## GS-062: Enlightenment political thought

Mains GS1 · World History · 18th–20th century

*Goal:* Explain Enlightenment political thought and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Enlightenment thinkers debated reason, rights and legitimate authority. Their ideas challenged inherited privilege, but differed on sovereignty, property and the scope of political equality.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Enlightenment arguments made reason, consent and rights standards for judging inherited political authority. Thinkers disagreed over property, representation, sovereignty and who counted as an equal political subject. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Do not present the Enlightenment as one doctrine or assume universal language immediately produced universal inclusion.
