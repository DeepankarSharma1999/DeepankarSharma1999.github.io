## CT-023: Syllogisms and logical deductions

Prelims CSAT (qualifying) · CSAT · Reasoning / decision making

*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Syllogisms and logical deductions and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Syllogisms and logical deductions is a reasoning / decision making skill. List only the facts that are given, symbolize the constraints, and draw a small table, diagram or Venn set. A valid conclusion must follow in every arrangement allowed by the statements. For decision questions, identify stakeholders, legality, urgency and the least harmful workable action.

### Timed application

Attempt this before viewing the hint: All judges are graduates. Some graduates are artists. Must some judges be artists? Give yourself two minutes, choose an answer and write one line explaining why. Then compare with: Suggested answer / check: No; intersection is not guaranteed. Retry a similar item after three study days and explain why every rejected option fails.
