## GS-357: Organised crime and terror financing

Mains GS3 + Prelims GS1 · Security · Internal security

*Goal:* Explain Organised crime and terror financing and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Organized crime seeks sustained illicit profit through networks and coercion. Terrorist organizations may use smuggling, extortion, forged documents or other criminal services to obtain money and mobility. Their objectives differ, even when their supply chains overlap.

### How to apply it

Following financial and logistical links can reveal enabling networks without assuming every criminal is ideologically committed. Prevention combines investigation, customs enforcement, anti-corruption measures and international cooperation. Seizure values alone are an incomplete success measure: networks may shift routes or replace intermediaries. Distinguish evidence of a particular transaction from proof of an enduring organizational alliance.
