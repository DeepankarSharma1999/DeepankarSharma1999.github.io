## PH-121: Macrostates and microstates

Physics Paper I · Physics · Statistical physics

*Goal:* Understand Macrostates and microstates, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Macrostates and microstates belongs to Statistical physics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Name the ensemble, constraints and particle statistics. Build the multiplicity or partition function before taking averages. Maxwell–Boltzmann, Bose–Einstein and Fermi–Dirac distributions have different occupancy assumptions; verify normalization and the classical limit.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive the main physical relation(s) governing macrostates and microstates. Define every symbol, state approximations and provide a labelled sketch where useful. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: Using a two-level or ideal-gas model, explain the assumptions and evaluate a limit for macrostates and microstates.
