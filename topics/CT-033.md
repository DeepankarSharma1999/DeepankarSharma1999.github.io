## CT-033: Tables, charts and graphs

Prelims CSAT (qualifying) · CSAT · Reasoning / decision making

*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Tables, charts and graphs and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Tables, charts and graphs is a reasoning / decision making skill. List only the facts that are given, symbolize the constraints, and draw a small table, diagram or Venn set. A valid conclusion must follow in every arrangement allowed by the statements. For decision questions, identify stakeholders, legality, urgency and the least harmful workable action.

### Timed application

Attempt this before viewing the hint: A bar chart reports 40, 60, 100 items in three categories. What share belongs to the largest category? Give yourself two minutes, choose an answer and write one line explaining why. Then compare with: Suggested answer / check: 50%. Retry a similar item after three study days and explain why every rejected option fails.
