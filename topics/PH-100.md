## PH-100: Normal/anomalous dispersion

Physics Paper I · Physics · EM waves and radiation

*Goal:* Understand Normal/anomalous dispersion, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Normal/anomalous dispersion belongs to EM waves and radiation. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Start from Maxwell’s equations and the material relations. For a plane wave verify E, B and propagation direction, then use the Poynting vector for energy flow. At an interface apply tangential boundary conditions; for radiation laws state the variable and integration range.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive the main physical relation(s) governing normal/anomalous dispersion. Define every symbol, state approximations and provide a labelled sketch where useful. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: For plane wave fields or a radiating body, formulate an observable for normal/anomalous dispersion and show the relevant low/high-frequency limit.
