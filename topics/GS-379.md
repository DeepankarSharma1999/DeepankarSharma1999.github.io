## GS-379: EI in conflict resolution and administration

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Attitude, aptitude and emotional intelligence

*Goal:* Explain EI in conflict resolution and administration and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Emotional intelligence helps administration when conflict, stress or distrust threaten communication. Active listening separates a person's concern from the intensity of its expression. Self-regulation helps an official avoid retaliatory reactions, while empathy makes underlying barriers more visible.

### How to apply it

Use these skills with clear boundaries and accountable decisions. Acknowledging frustration does not promise an unlawful benefit; explaining constraints respectfully can preserve trust while refusing a request. In negotiation, identify interests rather than only positions and test options that meet legitimate needs. Emotional skills become unethical if used to manipulate vulnerable people, so effectiveness must remain connected to honesty, consent and public duty.

### Worked example

During a tense land meeting, summarize residents' concerns accurately before explaining the lawful compensation process and review options.

### Recall

- - **q:** Why does empathy not require granting every demand?
  - **a:** An official can understand hardship while remaining bound by law, fairness and the rights of others.

### Common trap

Using de-escalation to silence a valid grievance without addressing it.
