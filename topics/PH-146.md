## PH-146: LS coupling and JJ coupling

Physics Paper II · Physics · Atomic and molecular

*Goal:* Understand LS coupling and JJ coupling, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

LS coupling and JJ coupling belongs to Atomic and molecular. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Identify the angular momenta and coupling scheme before assigning term symbols or selection rules. Separate rotational, vibrational and electronic energy scales. For spectra, connect level differences to frequency and explain intensity through population and transition probability.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive the main physical relation(s) governing ls coupling and jj coupling. Define every symbol, state approximations and provide a labelled sketch where useful. After that attempt the second application without notes: For a diatomic molecule or hydrogen-like atom, calculate/identify the spectral transition governed by ls coupling and jj coupling and its selection rule.
