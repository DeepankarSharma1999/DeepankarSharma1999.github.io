## GS-333: India–Sri Lanka and Maldives relations

Mains GS2 + Prelims GS1 · International Relations · India and world

*Goal:* Explain the strategic importance and distinct concerns of Sri Lanka and Maldives.

### Two island relationships, different issues

Sri Lanka and Maldives lie near important Indian Ocean sea routes. Maritime safety, connectivity, tourism and disaster assistance connect their interests with India. Sri Lanka also has a distinct history involving ethnic reconciliation and the rights and aspirations of its Tamil population. Maldives faces the vulnerabilities of a dispersed island economy and dependence on reliable external connections.

### Cooperate without overlooking local politics

Development projects, emergency assistance, fisheries management and training can support trust. Projects must remain transparent, financially viable and responsive to local priorities. In Sri Lanka, fishermen’s livelihoods and marine conservation require practical dialogue. Across both relationships, domestic political debate can change perceptions of external involvement, so continuity depends on institutions and public benefit, not only relationships between leaders.
