## PH-187: Band theory: conductor semiconductor insulator

Physics Paper II · Physics · Solid state physics

*Goal:* Understand Band theory: conductor semiconductor insulator, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Band theory: conductor semiconductor insulator belongs to Solid state physics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Connect microscopic order to the measurement: lattice plus basis gives structure, reciprocal spacing controls diffraction, and electronic states form bands. State the approximation and temperature regime. Distinguish a material property from the instrument used to infer it.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Explain energy band formation and distinguish metals, semiconductors and insulators. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: How does temperature usually change conductivity in an intrinsic semiconductor versus a metal?
