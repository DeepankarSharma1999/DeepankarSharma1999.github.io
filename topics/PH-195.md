## PH-195: PN junction basics and characteristics

Physics Paper II · Physics · Devices and electronics

*Goal:* Understand PN junction basics and characteristics, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

PN junction basics and characteristics belongs to Devices and electronics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Start from the device characteristic and operating region, then build the circuit relation. Mark bias polarity, current direction and reference ground. For logic, write the truth table before simplifying; for amplifiers distinguish voltage gain, power gain, feedback and saturation.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive Shockley ideal diode current-voltage relation and its assumptions. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: For an ideal diode, what is I/Is when V=(kT/q)ln(11)?
