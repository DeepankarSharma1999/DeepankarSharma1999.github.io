## PH-138: Rectangular barrier and tunnelling

Physics Paper II · Physics · Quantum mechanics

*Goal:* Understand Rectangular barrier and tunnelling, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Rectangular barrier and tunnelling belongs to Quantum mechanics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Write the state, operator and boundary conditions. Normalize before calculating probabilities or expectation values. Eigenvalues are measurement outcomes; a general state is a superposition. Check Hermiticity, units and limiting behaviour rather than treating a memorized wavefunction as universal.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive transmission dependence on barrier thickness for E<V0. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: Explain how doubling barrier width changes tunnelling probability in WKB approximation.
