## GS-220: RBI functions and Monetary Policy Committee

Prelims GS1 + Mains GS3 · Economy · Basic macro and monetary

*Goal:* Explain RBI functions and Monetary Policy Committee and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### The RBI's functions

India's central bank began in 1935. NCERT lists its core roles: it alone issues currency; it regulates money supply through the bank rate, open market operations and reserve ratios; it is banker to the government; it holds the country's foreign exchange reserves; and it is banker to commercial banks. Because it stands ready to lend to banks when they need funds, it is called the lender of last resort. Its policy tools are quantitative (CRR, bank rate, open market operations, repo) or qualitative (moral suasion, margin requirements). NCERT adds that repo and reverse repo operations at various maturities have now become the RBI's main monetary policy tool.

### How the MPC works

The Monetary Policy Committee is a statutory body under Section 45ZB of the RBI Act. The December 2021 minutes show its make-up: three external members drawn from research institutions, an RBI executive director nominated by the Central Board, the Deputy Governor in charge of monetary policy, and the Governor in the chair. Members vote individually, and dissent is recorded — in that meeting the repo decision was unanimous but one member did not support continuing the accommodative stance. Section 45ZL requires minutes, with each member's vote and statement, to be released on the fourteenth day after the meeting. Decisions are framed around a CPI inflation target of 4% with a ±2% band, while supporting growth.

### Transmission in 2025

The Economic Survey 2025-26 traces a full easing cycle. With inflation falling, the MPC lowered the repo rate by 100 basis points between April and December 2025, to 5.25%, and shifted its stance to neutral in June 2025. The RBI kept liquidity in surplus, so the weighted average call rate — the operating target — averaged slightly below the repo rate. The Survey calls transmission robust: between April and November 2025 the weighted average lending rate on fresh rupee loans fell 64 bps to 8.71% and on outstanding loans 56 bps to 9.21%, still less than the 100-bp policy cut. It credits surplus liquidity for this pass-through.

### Worked example

Answer frame (transmission, 150 words): define the repo rate and MPC → chain: repo → call money rate (operating target) → bank deposit and lending rates → consumption and investment → inflation. Evidence: 100-bp repo cut in 2025 vs 64-bp fall in fresh-loan rates (Apr–Nov 2025). Supporting conditions: surplus liquidity kept the call rate about 8 bps below repo in FY26, and outstanding-loan rates fell 56 bps (Apr–Nov 2025). Conclude: transmission was robust but smaller than the policy cut, so liquidity management matters alongside the rate decision.

### Recall

- - **q:** Who sets India's policy repo rate?
  - **a:** The Monetary Policy Committee constituted under Section 45ZB of the RBI Act.
- - **q:** When must MPC minutes be published?
  - **a:** On the fourteenth day after the meeting (Section 45ZL).
- - **q:** What is the operating target of monetary policy?
  - **a:** The weighted average call rate.

### Common trap

Assuming the Union Cabinet or Finance Commission fixes the repo rate: the MPC decides it by vote, within the statutory framework of the RBI Act.
