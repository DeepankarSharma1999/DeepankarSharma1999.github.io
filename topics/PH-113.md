## PH-113: Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution

Physics Paper I · Physics · Thermodynamics

*Goal:* Understand Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Maxwell-Boltzmann velocity distribution belongs to Thermodynamics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Define the system, path and sign convention. Use dU=δQ−δW while remembering that heat and work are path quantities. Entropy, free energy and Maxwell relations require the relevant constraints; check reversible limits before applying ideal-gas shortcuts.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive the main physical relation(s) governing maxwell-boltzmann velocity distribution. Define every symbol, state approximations and provide a labelled sketch where useful. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: In a quasi-static process involving maxwell-boltzmann velocity distribution, determine signs and expressions for Q, W, ΔU or ΔS; state constraints.
