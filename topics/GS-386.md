## GS-386: Corporate governance

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Thinkers, administration and probity

*Goal:* Explain Corporate governance and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Corporate governance concerns how companies are directed, controlled and held accountable. Boards, management, shareholders and other stakeholders have different roles. Ethical governance addresses conflicts of interest, truthful reporting and responsible risk-taking, not only formal compliance.

### How to apply it

Agency problems arise when managers control resources while bearing different incentives from owners or affected parties. Independent scrutiny, audit quality, disclosure and whistleblower channels can reduce these risks, but independence must function in practice. Stakeholder concerns include workers, customers, creditors and communities exposed to external costs. Short-term profit can coexist with hidden long-term liabilities, so governance should evaluate incentives and the reliability of information reaching decision makers.
