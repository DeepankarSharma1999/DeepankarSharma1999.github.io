## CT-036: Timed mixed CSAT mock and error analysis

Prelims CSAT (qualifying) · CSAT · Reasoning / decision making

*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Timed mixed CSAT mock and error analysis and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Timed mixed CSAT mock and error analysis is a reasoning / decision making skill. List only the facts that are given, symbolize the constraints, and draw a small table, diagram or Venn set. A valid conclusion must follow in every arrangement allowed by the statements. For decision questions, identify stakeholders, legality, urgency and the least harmful workable action.

### Timed application

Attempt this before viewing the hint: In a 10-question timed mock you attempt 8, answer 6 correct and 2 wrong. What is accuracy on attempts? Give yourself two minutes, choose an answer and write one line explaining why. Then compare with: Suggested answer / check: 75%; separately note exam scoring depends on paper rules. Retry a similar item after three study days and explain why every rejected option fails.
