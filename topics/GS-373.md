## GS-373: Social influence and persuasion

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Attitude, aptitude and emotional intelligence

*Goal:* Explain Social influence and persuasion and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Social influence changes judgments or behaviour through others' presence, norms or authority. Persuasion seeks change through communication and reasons; coercion relies on threats or force. Credibility, audience concerns and the quality of evidence shape a message's effects.

### How to apply it

Ethical persuasion respects autonomy and presents material facts honestly. A trusted local messenger can explain a service more effectively than a distant slogan, but trust should not be exploited through false claims. Tailor language and examples without concealing risks or manipulating vulnerabilities. Evaluate outcomes beyond immediate compliance: lasting understanding and voluntary participation matter when the objective requires continuing cooperation.
