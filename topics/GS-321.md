## GS-321: Persons with disabilities: rights and access

Mains GS2 · Social Justice · Rights, welfare and services

*Goal:* Explain disability rights through access, accommodation and participation.

### Change the environment as well as support the person

Disability-related disadvantage can arise when an impairment interacts with inaccessible buildings, communication or attitudes. The Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 adopts a rights-based framework including non-discrimination and reasonable accommodation. A ramp, accessible information or adapted examination arrangement can remove a barrier that otherwise prevents equal participation.

### Universal design and individual accommodation

Universal design anticipates diverse users from the beginning; reasonable accommodation provides necessary adjustments for a particular person when required. Accessible websites, clear signage and step-free routes benefit many people. Consult users and test actual service completion. Certification or a device alone does not solve inaccessible transport, hostile treatment or an unsuitable workplace.
