## GS-367: Moral lessons from administrators and reformers

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Ethics and human interface

*Goal:* Explain Moral lessons from administrators and reformers and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Leaders, reformers and administrators offer ethical lessons through choices under particular constraints. A useful example identifies the conflict, action and principle rather than relying on a famous name. Admiration does not remove the need to examine evidence, context or limitations.

### How to apply it

Compare different approaches without flattening them into one message. Gandhi's emphasis on means and ends, Ambedkar's concern with constitutional morality and social equality, and traditions of administrative impartiality illuminate different problems. Apply a lesson by explaining the analogy and its limits. A contemporary officer cannot simply copy a historical action without considering current law, institutional responsibility and affected citizens.
