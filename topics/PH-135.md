## PH-135: Finite square well

Physics Paper II · Physics · Quantum mechanics

*Goal:* Understand Finite square well, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Finite square well belongs to Quantum mechanics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Write the state, operator and boundary conditions. Normalize before calculating probabilities or expectation values. Eigenvalues are measurement outcomes; a general state is a superposition. Check Hermiticity, units and limiting behaviour rather than treating a memorized wavefunction as universal.

After that attempt the second application without notes: For a well-defined state or potential, show how to compute the observable associated with finite square well using normalized states.
