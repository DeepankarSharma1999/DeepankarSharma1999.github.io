## PH-134: Infinite square well and quantisation

Physics Paper II · Physics · Quantum mechanics

*Goal:* Understand Infinite square well and quantisation, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Infinite square well and quantisation belongs to Quantum mechanics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Write the state, operator and boundary conditions. Normalize before calculating probabilities or expectation values. Eigenvalues are measurement outcomes; a general state is a superposition. Check Hermiticity, units and limiting behaviour rather than treating a memorized wavefunction as universal.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Solve the infinite one-dimensional square well with boundary conditions. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: For width L, calculate E2/E1 and where first excited ψ(x) has a node.
