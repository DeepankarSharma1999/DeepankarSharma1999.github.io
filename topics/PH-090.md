## PH-090: Quality factor and bandwidth

Physics Paper I · Physics · Current electricity

*Goal:* Understand Quality factor and bandwidth, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Quality factor and bandwidth belongs to Current electricity. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Label currents and polarities before writing Kirchhoff equations. For transients find initial and final conditions plus the time constant; for sinusoidal steady state use complex impedance and report measurable RMS values. Resonance and bandwidth must use the same circuit convention.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive the main physical relation(s) governing quality factor and bandwidth. Define every symbol, state approximations and provide a labelled sketch where useful. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: In a linear lumped circuit illustrating quality factor and bandwidth, write all governing relations and solve for the requested current/voltage ratio.
