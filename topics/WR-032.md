## WR-032: Usage: articles and prepositions

English Paper B (qualifying) · Writing/Language · Reading and writing

*Goal:* Practise Usage: articles and prepositions with a clear structure, a strict word limit and a short revision pass.

### Technique

Read the instruction and word limit before drafting. Preserve the author’s meaning, organize one idea per sentence, and prefer precise plain English. In comprehension, separate what the passage states from what it permits you to infer. In writing, revise agreement, tense, articles, prepositions and punctuation. For “Usage: articles and prepositions”, write a tiny plan first: task, central idea, supporting points and final check. This reduces working-memory load and makes it easier to resume after an interruption.

### Apply now

Complete this exercise: Write a 120–150-word response illustrating usage: articles and prepositions. Check grammar, clarity and word economy. Use the self-check only after drafting: Self-check: exact meaning, correct grammar, adequate structure and strict word limit. Then perform a second pass for clarity and format. If the task is a full paper or PYQ set, divide it into sections and record time used rather than trying to review an entire PDF at once.
