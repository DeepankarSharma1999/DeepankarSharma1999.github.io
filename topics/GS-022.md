## GS-022: Medieval economy, commerce and society

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Medieval India

*Goal:* Explain Medieval economy, commerce and society and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Medieval Indian economies combined agriculture, artisanal production and local and long-distance trade. Towns depended on hinterlands, revenue demand and commercial networks rather than royal courts alone.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Agrarian surplus supported courts, towns and craft production, while merchants connected inland markets with maritime routes. Credit, brokerage and revenue demand linked cultivators, artisans, traders, zamindars and the state. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Do not infer economic decline merely from political conflict or describe towns as independent of rural hinterlands.

### Worked example

Textile centres linked spinners, weavers, merchants and overseas demand.
