## CT-013: Profit, loss and discount

Prelims CSAT (qualifying) · CSAT · Quantitative aptitude

*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Profit, loss and discount and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Profit, loss and discount is a quantitative aptitude skill. Translate words into variables, write the relation before calculating, and estimate the answer range. Keep fractions until the last step when possible. If options are available, test them only after understanding the relation; record whether an error came from concept, setup, arithmetic or time pressure.

### Timed application

Attempt this before viewing the hint: An item cost ₹800 and sold for ₹920. Calculate profit percent. Give yourself two minutes, choose an answer and write one line explaining why. Then compare with: Suggested answer / check: 15%. Retry a similar item after three study days and explain why every rejected option fails.

### Worked example

Use the first exercise as the worked checkpoint. Record the chosen method, answer, elapsed time and error category. The goal is a repeatable process, not a lucky option.

### Recall

- - **q:** What is your first move on a Profit, loss and discount question?
  - **a:** Define variables and write the relation before calculating.

### Common trap

Choosing a familiar-looking option without proving that it follows from the stated information.
