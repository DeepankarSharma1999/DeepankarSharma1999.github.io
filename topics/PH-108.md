## PH-108: Isothermal/adiabatic/isobaric/isochoric processes

Physics Paper I · Physics · Thermodynamics

*Goal:* Understand Isothermal/adiabatic/isobaric/isochoric processes, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Isothermal/adiabatic/isobaric/isochoric processes belongs to Thermodynamics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Define the system, path and sign convention. Use dU=δQ−δW while remembering that heat and work are path quantities. Entropy, free energy and Maxwell relations require the relevant constraints; check reversible limits before applying ideal-gas shortcuts.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive heat, work and entropy changes in ideal-gas isothermal and adiabatic processes. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: An ideal gas expands isothermally to twice its initial volume. Calculate work in terms of nRT.
