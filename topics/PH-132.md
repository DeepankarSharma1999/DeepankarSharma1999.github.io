## PH-132: Uncertainty principle

Physics Paper II · Physics · Quantum mechanics

*Goal:* Understand Uncertainty principle, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Uncertainty principle belongs to Quantum mechanics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Write the state, operator and boundary conditions. Normalize before calculating probabilities or expectation values. Eigenvalues are measurement outcomes; a general state is a superposition. Check Hermiticity, units and limiting behaviour rather than treating a memorized wavefunction as universal.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive the position–momentum uncertainty inequality and explain what it does not mean. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: If Δx=10⁻¹⁰ m, give minimum Δp in terms of ℏ.

### Worked example

Worked route: identify the requested result in “Derive the position–momentum uncertainty inequality and explain what it does not mean.”; select the governing principle; state assumptions; carry the algebra without skipping vector directions or boundary conditions; then interpret the result physically. Use the supplied self-check rubric: Assessment: principle and diagram → algebra/derivation → assumptions → units/limiting case.

### Recall

- - **q:** Which governing equation and assumptions control Uncertainty principle?
  - **a:** State the equation, define every symbol, name the approximation or boundary condition, and give one limiting case. Then reproduce the first derivation or calculation from the practice tab.

### Common trap

Substituting into a remembered formula before fixing the system, sign convention, approximation and units.
