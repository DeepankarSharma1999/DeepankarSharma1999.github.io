## GS-381: Western ethical thinkers and their ideas

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Thinkers, administration and probity

*Goal:* Explain Western ethical thinkers and their ideas and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Western ethical theories offer different evaluative frameworks. Utilitarianism evaluates consequences for overall welfare; Kantian approaches emphasize duty and persons as ends; virtue ethics examines character and practical judgment. Rawls's account of justice asks how fair institutions could be chosen under impartial conditions.

### How to apply it

These theories can illuminate different weaknesses in one proposal. A policy maximizing aggregate benefit may impose unjust burdens on a minority; a rigid rule may ignore serious avoidable harm. Do not claim the theories always agree or that one simple calculation resolves every conflict. Explain the principle being used, the relevant evidence and the interests that might be overlooked. Theoretical vocabulary matters only when it improves the reasoning.
