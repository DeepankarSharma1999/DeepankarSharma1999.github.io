## PH-124: Bose-Einstein distribution

Physics Paper I · Physics · Statistical physics

*Goal:* Understand Bose-Einstein distribution, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Bose-Einstein distribution belongs to Statistical physics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Name the ensemble, constraints and particle statistics. Build the multiplicity or partition function before taking averages. Maxwell–Boltzmann, Bose–Einstein and Fermi–Dirac distributions have different occupancy assumptions; verify normalization and the classical limit.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive Bose–Einstein mean occupancy and show the low-occupancy limit. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: Explain why a boson chemical potential is bounded by the ground-state energy.

### Worked example

Worked route: identify the requested result in “Derive Bose–Einstein mean occupancy and show the low-occupancy limit.”; select the governing principle; state assumptions; carry the algebra without skipping vector directions or boundary conditions; then interpret the result physically. Use the supplied self-check rubric: Assessment: principle and diagram → algebra/derivation → assumptions → units/limiting case.
