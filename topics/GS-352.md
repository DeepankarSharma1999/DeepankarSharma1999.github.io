## GS-352: Extremism and underdevelopment linkages

Mains GS3 + Prelims GS1 · Security · Internal security

*Goal:* Explain Extremism and underdevelopment linkages and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Development and extremism interact through governance, exclusion, livelihoods and political grievances. Poverty alone neither explains nor predicts organized violence. Mobilizing organizations, coercion, geography and access to justice influence whether grievances become part of an extremist movement.

### How to apply it

Development projects can reduce vulnerability when they improve legitimate access to services and voice. Poorly managed land acquisition or unaccountable force can instead deepen grievances. A sound analysis separates structural conditions, immediate triggers and organizational strategies. It evaluates security, rights, public-service delivery and political inclusion together, without excusing violence or assuming every deprived community supports it.
