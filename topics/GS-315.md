## GS-315: Role of charities, donors and institutional actors

Mains GS2 · Governance · Policy implementation and delivery

*Goal:* Evaluate the contributions and accountability of non-state welfare institutions.

### Who supplies what?

Charities can mobilize donations and volunteers; foundations can finance research or services; universities contribute evidence and trained people. Institutional actors also include hospitals, cooperatives and professional associations. Their flexibility can help test an idea before large public adoption. Their preferences, however, may reflect donors rather than the priorities of the least visible communities.

### Keep public purpose clear

Assess who chooses beneficiaries, controls funds and evaluates results. Publish selection rules and protect recipients’ dignity and data. A donor-funded clinic can complement government services, but uncertain donations make it a fragile substitute for an entitlement. Partnerships need continuity plans, conflict-of-interest safeguards and grievance channels. Beneficiaries should have a voice rather than being treated only as grateful recipients.
