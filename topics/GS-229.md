## GS-229: Poverty measurement and inequality

Prelims GS1 + Mains GS3 · Economy · Basic macro and monetary

*Goal:* Explain Poverty measurement and inequality and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### How the national MPI is built

Income-based poverty measures miss deprivations that money totals do not capture. NITI Aayog's national Multidimensional Poverty Index adapts the global MPI of OPHI and UNDP using the Alkire-Foster method. Health, education and living standards are its three dimensions, each carrying equal weight, tracked through 12 indicators: the ten global ones plus maternal health and bank accounts. Each person gets a weighted deprivation score; anyone at or above 33.33% is counted as poor. Two sub-indices follow: the headcount ratio, the share of people who are poor, and intensity, the average deprivation score among the poor. Their product is the MPI value, so it falls if fewer people are poor or if the poor are less deprived.

### What the numbers show

NITI Aayog's 2023 progress review, comparing NFHS-4 (2015-16) with NFHS-5 (2019-21), finds the poor share fell from 24.85% to 14.96%, a 9.89-point drop equivalent to about 135.5 million people, and intensity fell from 47.14% to 44.39%. Rural poverty fell faster, from 32.59% to 19.28%, than urban, from 8.65% to 5.27%. The Economic Survey 2025-26 adds income-based evidence: after the World Bank moved its international line up to USD 3.00 per day in June 2025, India's 2022-23 extreme poverty was 5.3% and lower-middle-income poverty 23.9%, using HCES 2022-23 data. It also reports the MPI headcount falling from 55.3% in 2005-06.

### Inequality is a different question

Poverty counts people below a threshold; inequality describes how a total is shared. NCERT's illustration makes the gap concrete: an economy of 100 people each earning Rs 10 has output of Rs 1,000; if next year 90 earn Rs 9 and 10 earn Rs 20, output rises to Rs 1,010 even though nine in ten people are worse off. Growth figures, and even average or per-capita income, therefore cannot settle whether most citizens gained. The Economic Survey 2025-26 frames state policy around equal opportunity, eliminating absolute poverty and preventing inequality from rising, using income support, social protection and education as equalisers. In Mains, pair a poverty measure with a distribution measure.

### Worked example

Suppose 15% of people are MPI-poor (H = 0.15) and their average deprivation score is 44% (A = 0.44). MPI = 0.15 × 0.44 = 0.066. If a programme lowers A to 0.40 with H unchanged, MPI falls to 0.060 — an improvement the headcount alone would hide.

### Recall

- - **q:** What are the national MPI's dimensions and indicator count?
  - **a:** Health, education and standard of living (equally weighted), 12 indicators.
- - **q:** What deprivation score makes a person MPI-poor?
  - **a:** 33.33% or more.
- - **q:** What was the World Bank's international poverty line revision in June 2025?
  - **a:** It was raised to USD 3.00 per day (2021 PPP), up from USD 2.15.

### Common trap

Treating the MPI headcount as an income-poverty rate: MPI measures overlapping non-monetary deprivations from NFHS data, not consumption below a money line.
