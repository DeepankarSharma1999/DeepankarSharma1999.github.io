## GS-064: Industrial Revolution and social consequences

Mains GS1 · World History · 18th–20th century

*Goal:* Explain Industrial Revolution and social consequences and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Industrialization shifted production toward mechanized factories and new energy sources. Urbanization, wage labour and class conflict followed unevenly, reshaping work and social relationships.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Industrialisation joined mechanisation and new energy to factory discipline, but hand production persisted and adapted. Urban growth and wage labour created new wealth alongside insecure work, poor housing and collective labour organization. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Do not equate invention with instant economy-wide adoption or assume factories immediately eliminated handicrafts.
