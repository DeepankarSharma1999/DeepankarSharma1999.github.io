## PH-150: Rotational spectra of diatomic molecules

Physics Paper II · Physics · Atomic and molecular

*Goal:* Understand Rotational spectra of diatomic molecules, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Rotational spectra of diatomic molecules belongs to Atomic and molecular. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Identify the angular momenta and coupling scheme before assigning term symbols or selection rules. Separate rotational, vibrational and electronic energy scales. For spectra, connect level differences to frequency and explain intensity through population and transition probability.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive rigid-rotor rotational transition energies and selection rule. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: If B=2 cm⁻¹, find the wavenumber of J=0 to J=1 transition.

### Worked example

Worked route: identify the requested result in “Derive rigid-rotor rotational transition energies and selection rule.”; select the governing principle; state assumptions; carry the algebra without skipping vector directions or boundary conditions; then interpret the result physically.
