## GS-004: Mahajanapadas and rise of Magadha

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Ancient India

*Goal:* Explain Mahajanapadas and rise of Magadha and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Sixteen mahajanapadas are associated with early historic northern India. Magadha benefited from its riverine position, agricultural resources and political expansion; it was not powerful for one cause alone.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Build the topic around three defensible links. The sixteen mahajanapadas mark a phase of larger territorial states in northern India. Magadha combined fertile land, river routes, resources, fortified centres and sustained political expansion. Gana-sangha polities differed from monarchies but were not modern democracies based on universal citizenship. For a written answer or worked solution, state the governing idea, apply it to a concrete example, and finish with the limitation: Single-cause answers about iron, elephants or geography miss the interaction of resources, routes and statecraft.
