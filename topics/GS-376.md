## GS-376: Non-partisanship and public service orientation

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Attitude, aptitude and emotional intelligence

*Goal:* Explain Non-partisanship and public service orientation and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Non-partisanship means public authority is exercised independently of improper party preference. Dedication to public service concerns sustained commitment to lawful duties and citizens' welfare. Both permit professional advice and honest disagreement within legitimate institutional channels.

### How to apply it

Officials serve elected governments while retaining responsibilities to the Constitution and the public. Responsiveness to lawful policy differs from helping a party gain unfair electoral advantage. Consistent records, fair access and transparent criteria protect neutrality during political change. Dedication also includes competence, timely action and willingness to correct mistakes; long hours alone cannot prove that service is effective or fair.
