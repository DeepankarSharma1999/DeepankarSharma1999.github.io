## GS-021: Maratha state and Shivaji

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Medieval India

*Goal:* Explain Maratha state and Shivaji and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Shivaji built a Maratha state through forts, mobile warfare and administrative organization. Chauth and sardeshmukhi were revenue claims associated with later Maratha expansion as well.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Shivaji used a fort network, mobile forces and offices accountable to the centre while negotiating with powerful local interests. The later Maratha confederacy distributed power among major houses and cannot be projected unchanged back onto Shivaji's kingdom. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Do not equate chauth with ownership of the territory from which it was claimed.
