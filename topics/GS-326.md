## GS-326: Nutrition, hunger and malnutrition

Mains GS2 · Social Justice · Rights, welfare and services

*Goal:* Explain the distinct indicators and causes of malnutrition.

### Read indicators correctly

Stunting reflects low height for age and is associated with chronic growth constraints. Wasting concerns low weight for height and can indicate acute undernutrition. Underweight means low weight for age and can reflect several pathways. Micronutrient deficiency may coexist with adequate calories; overweight can also coexist with poor dietary quality. Nutrition is broader than food quantity alone.

### Break a reinforcing cycle

Poor diets, infection, unsafe water and inadequate care can reinforce one another. Maternal health and early childhood conditions affect growth and development. Effective action combines diverse food, appropriate feeding support, sanitation, accessible healthcare and social protection. Measurements should trigger qualified assessment and support rather than blame. Household averages can conceal unequal food distribution or unmet needs within families.
