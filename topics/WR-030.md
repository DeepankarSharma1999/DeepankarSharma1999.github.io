## WR-030: Précis: concision and word limit

English Paper B (qualifying) · Writing/Language · Reading and writing

*Goal:* Practise Précis: concision and word limit with a clear structure, a strict word limit and a short revision pass.

### Technique

Read the instruction and word limit before drafting. Preserve the author’s meaning, organize one idea per sentence, and prefer precise plain English. In comprehension, separate what the passage states from what it permits you to infer. In writing, revise agreement, tense, articles, prepositions and punctuation. For “Précis: concision and word limit”, write a tiny plan first: task, central idea, supporting points and final check. This reduces working-memory load and makes it easier to resume after an interruption.

### Apply now

Complete this exercise: Write a précis (about one-third length) and title for: “Reliable public institutions depend on clear rules. Yet rules alone cannot guarantee fairness. Officials must explain decisions, hear affected citizens and correct mistakes openly. Such transparency helps people understand why public choices were made.” Use the self-check only after drafting: Self-check: exact meaning, correct grammar, adequate structure and strict word limit. Then perform a second pass for clarity and format. If the task is a full paper or PYQ set, divide it into sections and record time used rather than trying to review an entire PDF at once.
