## GS-363: Determinants and consequences of human action

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Ethics and human interface

*Goal:* Explain Determinants and consequences of human action and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Human action has intentions, choices, circumstances and consequences. Ethical responsibility depends partly on knowledge, control and foreseeable effects; a good intention does not automatically excuse careless harm. Institutional incentives and social pressures can influence conduct without eliminating individual responsibility.

### How to apply it

Compare intended outcomes with predictable side effects and available alternatives. An officer who ignores obvious implementation risks cannot rely only on a benevolent objective. At the same time, hindsight should not convert every unforeseeable failure into misconduct. Sound judgment examines what could reasonably have been known and done at the time, then separates personal accountability from systemic causes requiring institutional repair.
