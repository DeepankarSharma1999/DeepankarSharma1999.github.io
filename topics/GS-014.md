## GS-014: Delhi Sultanate chronology and administration

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Medieval India

*Goal:* Explain Delhi Sultanate chronology and administration and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

The Delhi Sultanate comprised successive dynasties, not one unchanging regime. Iqta assignments linked revenue collection with service obligations, while central control varied across time and place.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Build the topic around three defensible links. The Sultanate sequence includes Mamluk, Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid and Lodi dynasties. Iqta assignments connected revenue rights with service, but their administration and degree of central control changed. Military technology, elite politics, agrarian revenue and provincial negotiation shaped expansion. For objective questions, classify each statement by process, chronology or scope before eliminating it. For a written answer or worked solution, state the governing idea, apply it to a concrete example, and finish with the limitation: An iqta was not automatically private hereditary ownership of land, and the Sultanate was not institutionally unchanged for three centuries.
