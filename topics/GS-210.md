## GS-210: Lokpal and Lokayuktas

Prelims GS1 + Mains GS2 · Polity · Institutions, law and politics

*Goal:* Explain Lokpal and Lokayuktas and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Origins: a two-tier ombudsman idea

The word Lokpal joins 'lok' (people) and 'pala' (protector) and is India's version of the Scandinavian ombudsman. The First Administrative Reforms Commission (1966) proposed two tiers: a Lokpal for the Centre and a Lokayukta in each State to deal with complaints against the remaining administrative machinery. Lokpal Bills were introduced repeatedly from 1968; the National Commission to Review the Working of the Constitution (2002) and the Second ARC wanted constitutional provisions for both institutions. After review by a Standing Committee and a Rajya Sabha Select Committee, whose agenda included creating Lokayuktas in the States, the Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act received assent on 1 January 2014 and came into force on 16 January 2014. It also helps implement the UN Convention against Corruption, ratified by India on 9 May 2011.

### Structure and jurisdiction

The Chairperson and members are appointed by the President on the recommendation of a selection committee led by the Prime Minister. They serve five years or until 70, and service conditions cannot be varied to their disadvantage. The Lokpal can inquire into corruption allegations against present or former Prime Ministers (with subject exclusions and full-bench approval), Union ministers, MPs, all groups of central public servants, office-bearers of centrally funded or controlled bodies, government-financed societies or trusts whose annual income exceeds one crore rupees (the limit notified in June 2016, counting only Central grants), and bodies receiving foreign contributions above ten lakh rupees a year. Removal requires a Supreme Court inquiry on a petition by at least one hundred MPs.

### Procedure and powers

On a complaint, the Lokpal may order a preliminary inquiry, referring complaints against Group A–D public servants to the Central Vigilance Commission, or direct investigation by an agency including the Delhi Special Police Establishment, to be completed within six months (extendable). Benches of at least three members decide on investigation, departmental action or closure. The Lokpal can grant sanction for prosecution, supervise the DSPE on referred matters (its investigating officers cannot be transferred without Lokpal approval), provisionally attach proceeds of corruption, recommend transfer or suspension of public servants, and protect records. Special Courts should finish trials within one year, extendable to two. False complaints attract imprisonment up to one year and fines up to one lakh rupees.

### Worked example

Statement check: 'An NGO receiving ₹15 lakh a year in foreign contributions is within Lokpal jurisdiction.' Correct — section 14(h) covers bodies receiving foreign contributions above ten lakh rupees per year.

### Recall

- - **q:** When did the Lokpal Act come into force?
  - **a:** 16 January 2014.
- - **q:** Who nominates the eminent jurist on the selection committee?
  - **a:** The President, on recommendation of the other committee members.
- - **q:** How many MPs must petition to start removal of a Lokpal member?
  - **a:** At least one hundred.

### Common trap

The Lokpal rests on statute: the Lokpal's own annual report credits the 2013 Act with giving it a statutory basis, even though the 2002 constitution-review commission had recommended constitutional provisions. The cited annual reports describe the Lokayukta tier only historically; the Act's State-level provisions are not on these pages, so do not quote them without the bare Act.
