## GS-308: Transparency and accountability mechanisms

Mains GS2 · Governance · Policy implementation and delivery

*Goal:* Connect public disclosure to answerability and enforceable correction.

### Visibility is the beginning

Transparency makes decisions, rules and spending understandable to the public. Accountability requires officials to explain decisions and face review, correction or consequences when standards are breached. A government can release thousands of inaccessible files while citizens remain unable to understand a procurement decision. Useful disclosure is timely, readable and connected to the decision citizens need to examine.

### Combine several checks

Legislative questions, independent audit, judicial review, grievance systems, social audits and internal supervision examine different failures. Clear responsibility prevents everyone from blaming another office. Effective systems record reasons, identify who acted, protect legitimate scrutiny and ensure follow-up. Privacy and necessary confidentiality require proportionate protection; they should not become blanket excuses for hiding institutional performance.
