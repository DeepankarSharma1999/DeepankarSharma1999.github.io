## GS-354: Terrorism and violent extremism

Mains GS3 + Prelims GS1 · Security · Internal security

*Goal:* Explain Terrorism and violent extremism and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Terrorism uses or threatens violence to intimidate wider audiences for political or ideological ends. Definitions vary across legal frameworks, so analytical description and statutory classification must be distinguished. Violent extremism concerns the adoption or support of violence in pursuit of extreme objectives.

### How to apply it

Prevention examines recruitment, propaganda, financing and enabling networks alongside immediate attacks. Responses need lawful intelligence, investigation and community trust, while protection of rights helps preserve legitimacy. One individual's ideology does not establish criminal responsibility without evidence of relevant conduct. A balanced answer separates causes, operational methods, effects and responses, avoiding collective blame against a religion or community.
