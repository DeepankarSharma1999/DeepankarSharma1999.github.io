## GS-395: Ethical case study: justified course of action

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Thinkers, administration and probity

*Goal:* Explain Ethical case study: justified course of action and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

A justified ethical decision states what will be done, why it is defensible and how it will be implemented. Reasons should connect facts, legal duties and relevant values rather than simply announce honesty or compassion. Acknowledge unavoidable costs and explain why the preferred option remains stronger.

### How to apply it

Implementation needs responsibilities, sequence, safeguards, communication and review. A good intention can fail if affected people cannot use the remedy or if no official owns follow-up. Build in monitoring and correction where facts remain uncertain. Transparency should be tailored to legitimate confidentiality and rights. In an examination answer, conclude with practical steps that follow from the analysis, instead of adding a generic list unrelated to the case.
