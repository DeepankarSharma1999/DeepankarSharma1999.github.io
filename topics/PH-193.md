## PH-193: High Tc superconductivity basics

Physics Paper II · Physics · Solid state physics

*Goal:* Understand High Tc superconductivity basics, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

High Tc superconductivity basics belongs to Solid state physics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Connect microscopic order to the measurement: lattice plus basis gives structure, reciprocal spacing controls diffraction, and electronic states form bands. State the approximation and temperature regime. Distinguish a material property from the instrument used to infer it.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive the main physical relation(s) governing high tc superconductivity basics. Define every symbol, state approximations and provide a labelled sketch where useful. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: Compare two materials/structures illustrating high tc superconductivity basics; predict the effect on a measurable electrical, structural or thermal property.
