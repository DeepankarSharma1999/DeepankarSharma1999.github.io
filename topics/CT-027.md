## CT-027: Blood relations

Prelims CSAT (qualifying) · CSAT · Reasoning / decision making

*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Blood relations and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Blood relations is a reasoning / decision making skill. List only the facts that are given, symbolize the constraints, and draw a small table, diagram or Venn set. A valid conclusion must follow in every arrangement allowed by the statements. For decision questions, identify stakeholders, legality, urgency and the least harmful workable action.

### Timed application

Attempt this before viewing the hint: Ravi is Meera’s brother; Meera is Sita’s mother. How is Ravi related to Sita? Give yourself two minutes, choose an answer and write one line explaining why. Then compare with: Suggested answer / check: Maternal uncle. Retry a similar item after three study days and explain why every rejected option fails.

### Worked example

Use the first exercise as the worked checkpoint. Record the chosen method, answer, elapsed time and error category. The goal is a repeatable process, not a lucky option.

### Recall

- - **q:** What is your first move on a Blood relations question?
  - **a:** List the given constraints and test only necessary conclusions.

### Common trap

Choosing a familiar-looking option without proving that it follows from the stated information.
