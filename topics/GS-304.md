## GS-304: Emerging medical technology

Prelims GS1 + Mains GS3 · Science · General science / technology

*Goal:* Evaluate a medical innovation using evidence, access and governance.

### Different technologies solve different problems

Medical imaging reveals internal structures; molecular diagnostics identify biological markers; telemedicine connects patients and clinicians across distance. Gene editing changes genetic sequences, while an AI decision-support system estimates patterns from data. These are distinct tools with different evidence requirements. Technical accuracy must be assessed in the population and clinical setting where the tool will actually operate.

### From invention to responsible use

Ask whether the technology improves a meaningful health outcome, causes harm, protects consent and remains affordable. A diagnostic test can be accurate yet produce many false positives when a condition is rare. Clinical trials, independent evaluation and trained staff connect invention to safe service. Digital systems also need privacy protections and an accessible route for people without reliable connectivity.
