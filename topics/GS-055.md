## GS-055: Linguistic reorganisation of states

Mains GS1 · History · Post-1947 India

*Goal:* Explain Linguistic reorganisation of states and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Linguistic reorganization sought administrative units responsive to language while balancing unity and other considerations. Andhra's formation in 1953 preceded the wider reorganization of 1956.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. The Andhra movement intensified the demand for state boundaries responsive to major language communities. The States Reorganisation Commission balanced language with unity, administration and economic viability before the 1956 settlement. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Linguistic reorganisation did not make every state monolingual or settle every boundary demand permanently.
