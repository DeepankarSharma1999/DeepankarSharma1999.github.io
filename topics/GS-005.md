## GS-005: Jainism and Buddhism: teachings and councils

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Ancient India

*Goal:* Explain Jainism and Buddhism: teachings and councils and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Jain traditions emphasize non-violence, non-possession and liberation through disciplined conduct. Buddhism teaches the Four Noble Truths and an Eightfold Path addressing suffering and its cessation.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Build the topic around three defensible links. Buddhism organizes its diagnosis around suffering, its origin, cessation and a path of practice. Jain traditions emphasize non-violence, non-possession, disciplined conduct and liberation from karmic bondage. Councils, sects and patronage belong to later institutional histories and should not be mixed with core teachings. For objective questions, classify each statement by process, chronology or scope before eliminating it. For a written answer or worked solution, state the governing idea, apply it to a concrete example, and finish with the limitation: Do not collapse Jain and Buddhist doctrines into one reform movement merely because both questioned ritual authority.

### Worked example

Compare Jain emphasis on rigorous ascetic restraint with the Buddhist Middle Way.
