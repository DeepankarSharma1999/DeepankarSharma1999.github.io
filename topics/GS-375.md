## GS-375: Integrity, objectivity and impartiality

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Attitude, aptitude and emotional intelligence

*Goal:* Explain Integrity, objectivity and impartiality and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Integrity is consistency between justified principles and conduct; objectivity requires decisions grounded in relevant evidence; impartiality requires fair treatment without improper preference. The concepts reinforce each other but address different risks. Personal sincerity alone does not establish objectivity.

### How to apply it

Apply them through explicit criteria, disclosure of conflicts, adequate records and openness to correction. An officer may consistently apply an unfair criterion, showing consistency without ethical integrity in the fuller public-service sense. Impartiality also permits justified differential treatment when relevant disadvantage requires support. Examine whether differences rest on legitimate policy objectives rather than connections, stereotypes or political influence.

### Worked example

Use published eligibility criteria and recorded evidence when selecting beneficiaries, while applying lawful accessibility accommodations.

### Recall

- - **q:** Why is identical treatment not always impartial treatment?
  - **a:** Relevant disadvantage or legally justified accommodation may require different assistance to secure fair access.

### Common trap

Equating personal conviction with evidence-based judgment.
