## GS-369: Ethical reasoning and moral dilemmas

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Ethics and human interface

*Goal:* Explain Ethical reasoning and moral dilemmas and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

An ethical dilemma involves competing legitimate obligations, not merely a choice between obvious honesty and corruption. Identify the values in conflict and distinguish real constraints from assumptions. Consequential, duty-based, rights-based and virtue approaches can reveal different aspects of the same case.

### How to apply it

A disciplined response identifies facts, affected people, legal limits and feasible options before ranking them. Consider who bears the harms, whether safeguards can reduce conflict and how the decision would withstand transparent explanation. Do not invent facts to make the preferred option effortless. A defensible conclusion acknowledges remaining costs and specifies implementation, monitoring and opportunities for correction.

### Worked example

Disclosing an inquiry may improve transparency but harm an innocent person's privacy; limited, anonymized disclosure can reduce the conflict.

### Recall

- - **q:** What distinguishes an ethical dilemma from simple misconduct?
  - **a:** A dilemma contains competing legitimate duties or values; straightforward personal gain through wrongdoing does not create that conflict.

### Common trap

Declaring every inconvenient decision a dilemma.
