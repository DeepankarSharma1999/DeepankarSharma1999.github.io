## CT-037: Interpersonal communication: active listening and conflict resolution

Prelims CSAT (qualifying) · CSAT · Reasoning / decision making

*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Interpersonal communication: active listening and conflict resolution and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Interpersonal communication: active listening and conflict resolution is a reasoning / decision making skill. List only the facts that are given, symbolize the constraints, and draw a small table, diagram or Venn set. A valid conclusion must follow in every arrangement allowed by the statements. For decision questions, identify stakeholders, legality, urgency and the least harmful workable action.

### Timed application

Attempt this before viewing the hint: Two stakeholders disagree on a proposed public scheme: one cites cost, another equitable access. Choose the best immediate response: (A) ignore both (B) listen and clarify priorities (C) announce a decision without discussion (D) argue with both. Give yourself two minutes, choose an answer and write one line explaining why. Then compare with: Suggested answer / check: B Retry a similar item after three study days and explain why every rejected option fails.
