## GS-056: Constitution-making and early institutions

Mains GS1 · History · Post-1947 India

*Goal:* Explain Constitution-making and early institutions and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

The Constituent Assembly combined committee work, debate and institutional design. The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949 and came generally into force on 26 January 1950.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. The Constituent Assembly worked through committees and open debate on federalism, rights, representation and social transformation. Adoption on 26 November 1949 and commencement on 26 January 1950 are separate constitutional dates. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Do not describe the Constitution as copied wholesale or confuse adoption, signing and commencement.

### Worked example

Adoption and commencement explain why two commemorative dates refer to different events.
