## GS-016: Sufism, orders and cultural influence

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Medieval India

*Goal:* Explain Sufism, orders and cultural influence and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Sufi silsilas linked teachers and disciples through spiritual lineages. Khanqahs could provide spaces of instruction, hospitality and social interaction; relations with rulers differed among orders.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Chishti khanqahs joined devotion to hospitality and public interaction, while orders differed in discipline and their distance from courts. Sama, vernacular expression and shrine networks helped Sufi ideas travel beyond a narrow scholarly circle. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Do not treat every silsila as anti-state or assume all Sufi practice was identical.
