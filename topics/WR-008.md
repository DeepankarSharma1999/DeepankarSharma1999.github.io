## WR-008: Balanced conclusion and way forward

Essay (Mains 250) · Writing/Language · Essay craft

*Goal:* Practise Balanced conclusion and way forward with a clear structure, a strict word limit and a short revision pass.

### Technique

Define the key terms in the prompt, write a one-sentence position, and map the argument across distinct dimensions. Each paragraph should make one claim, support it with a concrete example, examine a qualification, and link back to the thesis. Reserve time to test whether the conclusion answers the exact prompt. For “Balanced conclusion and way forward”, write a tiny plan first: task, central idea, supporting points and final check. This reduces working-memory load and makes it easier to resume after an interruption.

### Apply now

Complete this exercise: For “The strength of a society lies in the quality of its public institutions”, apply balanced conclusion and way forward to prepare a thesis, counterargument and five-part outline in 12 minutes. Use the self-check only after drafting: Checklist: interpretation → thesis → multiple dimensions → examples → balanced conclusion. Then perform a second pass for clarity and format. If the task is a full paper or PYQ set, divide it into sections and record time used rather than trying to review an entire PDF at once.

### Worked example

A strong response follows four visible moves: interpret the instruction, outline the answer, draft within the limit, and revise against the stated check. For transfer practice, use: Using “Balanced conclusion and way forward”, draft one well-structured introduction and two body paragraphs for a UPSC Mains-style essay.

### Recall

- - **q:** What four checks should you make for Balanced conclusion and way forward?
  - **a:** Instruction, central meaning, structure and language accuracy; also verify the required word limit or time limit.

### Common trap

Writing everything known about the theme instead of completing the precise language or argument task.
