## GS-300: Artificial intelligence, data and robotics

Prelims GS1 + Mains GS3 · Science · General science / technology

*Goal:* Explain how data becomes an AI prediction and a robotic action.

### Data → model → decision

Artificial intelligence performs tasks such as recognizing images, predicting demand or generating language. Machine learning learns patterns from examples rather than receiving every rule explicitly. Training adjusts model parameters; inference applies the trained model to new inputs. Data quality matters: missing groups, inaccurate labels or changed conditions can make apparently accurate systems fail outside their training setting.

### When prediction moves machinery

A robot combines sensors, computation and actuators: a camera observes, a controller selects an action and a motor moves. Robotics can use ordinary programmed control without machine learning. Public applications include crop monitoring and assistive devices. Evaluate reliability, privacy, explanation and human responsibility alongside speed; a fluent generated answer is not evidence that its factual claim is true.
