## GS-294: Genetics, DNA, RNA and biotechnology

Prelims GS1 + Mains GS3 · Science · General science / technology

*Goal:* Explain Genetics, DNA, RNA and biotechnology and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### DNA, RNA and the flow of information

DNA is a long polymer of deoxyribonucleotides; each nucleotide has a nitrogenous base, a pentose sugar (deoxyribose in DNA, ribose in RNA) and a phosphate. Purines are adenine and guanine; pyrimidines are cytosine, thymine and uracil, with uracil replacing thymine in RNA. Genome sizes range from 48,502 base pairs in bacteriophage lambda to about 4.6 million in E. coli and 3.3 billion in the haploid human set. The Watson-Crick double helix explained base pairing, and Crick's central dogma states that information passes from DNA to RNA to protein, though some viruses reverse the first step.

### Tools of recombinant DNA technology

Modern biotechnology rests on genetic engineering, which alters DNA or RNA and introduces it into hosts, and bioprocess engineering, which keeps sterile conditions for large-scale culture producing antibiotics, vaccines and enzymes. Unlike hybridisation, which carries along unwanted genes, recombinant methods transfer only selected genes. An alien DNA piece multiplies only if linked to an origin of replication, as in a plasmid vector. The first recombinant molecule linked an antibiotic-resistance gene to a native plasmid. Restriction endonucleases, the first being Hind II, recognise specific sequences and cut DNA; EcoRI is named after Escherichia coli RY 13. DNA ligase joins the cut fragments.

### Applications, regulation and ethics

Genetically modified organisms have had genes altered by manipulation; GM crops can tolerate abiotic stress, reduce pesticide use and post-harvest losses, and improve nutrition, as with vitamin A-rich golden rice. Bt crops carry cry genes from Bacillus thuringiensis whose protoxin turns toxic only in the alkaline insect gut. Human insulin is now made by producing chains A and B separately and joining them. Gene therapy began in 1990 for ADA deficiency using a retroviral vector. India's GEAC rules on GM research and release, and biopiracy concerns arose when a US company patented a Basmati-derived variety in 1997.
