## GS-325: Human resources, skilling and employment

Mains GS2 · Social Justice · Rights, welfare and services

*Goal:* Connect skills policy to labour demand and quality employment.

### Separate three labour-market questions

Labour-force participation asks who is working or seeking work. Unemployment concerns those in the labour force without work and seeking or available for it under the measure used. Job quality adds wages, security, safety and productivity. Training can improve employability, but it cannot create sufficient labour demand by itself.

### Make skills usable

Align training with actual tasks, include apprenticeships or practical experience and recognize existing skills. Employers need credible evidence of competence; workers need information, mobility and fair access. Women may face childcare and transport barriers even when trained. Track sustained employment and earnings after placement, not only certificates issued. Technological change makes continuing learning important across working lives.
