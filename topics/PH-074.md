## PH-074: Method of images

Physics Paper I · Physics · Electrostatics / magnetostatics

*Goal:* Understand Method of images, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Method of images belongs to Electrostatics / magnetostatics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Use symmetry before integration. Choose Gauss, Poisson/Laplace, images or multipoles according to geometry and boundary conditions. State which potential and normal-field components are continuous at an interface, and test the far-field behaviour.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Explain method of images and solve point charge near infinite grounded conducting plane. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: A charge q is at distance a from a grounded plane. Find the magnitude and direction of the force.

### Worked example

Worked route: identify the requested result in “Explain method of images and solve point charge near infinite grounded conducting plane.”; select the governing principle; state assumptions; carry the algebra without skipping vector directions or boundary conditions; then interpret the result physically. Use the supplied self-check rubric: Assessment: principle and diagram → algebra/derivation → assumptions → units/limiting case.
