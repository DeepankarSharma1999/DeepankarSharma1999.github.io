## PH-176: Fundamental particle classification

Physics Paper II · Physics · Nuclear and particle physics

*Goal:* Understand Fundamental particle classification, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Fundamental particle classification belongs to Nuclear and particle physics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Conserve charge, energy–momentum, angular momentum and the relevant quantum numbers. Use mass defect with consistent atomic or nuclear masses when finding Q values. For models, state both the phenomenon explained and the known limitation; do not mix interaction carriers with composite particles.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive the main physical relation(s) governing fundamental particle classification. Define every symbol, state approximations and provide a labelled sketch where useful. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: For a hypothetical reaction or particle system, determine conservation constraints for fundamental particle classification and one measurable consequence.
