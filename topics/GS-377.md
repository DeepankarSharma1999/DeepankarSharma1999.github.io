## GS-377: Compassion, tolerance and empathy

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Attitude, aptitude and emotional intelligence

*Goal:* Explain Compassion, tolerance and empathy and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Empathy seeks to understand another person's perspective and experience. Compassion adds concern and a motivation to help; tolerance accepts legitimate difference without necessarily agreeing. These qualities support respectful administration but do not replace evidence, fairness or lawful criteria.

### How to apply it

Understanding vulnerability can reveal barriers hidden by formally uniform procedures. A person unable to read may need assistance completing a form, not a lower standard of entitlement. Compassion should improve access and reduce avoidable harm without becoming favouritism. Tolerance has limits where conduct violates others' rights; respecting diversity does not require accepting coercion or abuse. Explain both the supportive action and its principled boundaries.

### Worked example

Provide accessible assistance to an eligible applicant with a disability while applying the same lawful entitlement criteria.

### Recall

- - **q:** How can compassion avoid becoming favouritism?
  - **a:** Address relevant barriers through fair, transparent support instead of granting benefits because of personal attachment.

### Common trap

Equating empathy with agreement or tolerance with acceptance of harm.
