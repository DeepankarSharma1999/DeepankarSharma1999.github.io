## GS-003: Vedic period and social institutions

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Ancient India

*Goal:* Explain Vedic period and social institutions and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Early Vedic society emphasized pastoral wealth and kinship. Later Vedic texts show expanding agriculture, territorial polities and more elaborate ritual and social differentiation.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Build the topic around three defensible links. Early Vedic evidence stresses mobile pastoral wealth and lineage; later evidence shows more agriculture and territorial rule. Varna ideals in normative texts do not by themselves describe every community or actual practice. Chronology, text type and archaeological setting must be kept separate when reconstructing social institutions. For objective questions, classify each statement by process, chronology or scope before eliminating it. For a written answer or worked solution, state the governing idea, apply it to a concrete example, and finish with the limitation: Do not project the later, rigid form of every institution backward into the earliest Vedic period.
