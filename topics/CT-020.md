## CT-020: Geometry, mensuration and basic trigonometry

Prelims CSAT (qualifying) · CSAT · Quantitative aptitude

*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Geometry, mensuration and basic trigonometry and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Geometry, mensuration and basic trigonometry is a quantitative aptitude skill. Translate words into variables, write the relation before calculating, and estimate the answer range. Keep fractions until the last step when possible. If options are available, test them only after understanding the relation; record whether an error came from concept, setup, arithmetic or time pressure.

### Timed application

Attempt this before viewing the hint: A circle has radius 7 cm. Find its area using π=22/7. Give yourself two minutes, choose an answer and write one line explaining why. Then compare with: Suggested answer / check: 154 cm². Retry a similar item after three study days and explain why every rejected option fails.

### Worked example

Use the first exercise as the worked checkpoint. Record the chosen method, answer, elapsed time and error category. The goal is a repeatable process, not a lucky option.
