## GS-346: NDMA, SDMA, NDRF and institutional roles

Mains GS3 · Disaster Management · Risk management

*Goal:* Separate disaster-policy institutions from operational response forces.

### Know the levels of responsibility

The Disaster Management Act, 2005 established a national-to-local institutional framework. NDMA is chaired by the Prime Minister; State Disaster Management Authorities are chaired by Chief Ministers. District arrangements coordinate local planning and response through designated authorities. Policy, guidelines, preparedness and implementation occur across these levels, so disaster management is not performed by one central organization alone.

### A force is one part of a wider system

The National Disaster Response Force provides specialist operational response capability. State responders, police, fire services, health workers, local bodies and communities remain essential. Logistics, information and clear incident coordination connect their work. The abbreviation NDRF can also refer to a disaster response fund in financial contexts; identify whether a question concerns personnel or financing.
