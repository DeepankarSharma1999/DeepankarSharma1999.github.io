## GS-384: Rule-based and conscience-based decisions

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Thinkers, administration and probity

*Goal:* Explain Rule-based and conscience-based decisions and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Rules promote consistency, predictability and protection against arbitrary power. Conscience can alert a person to unfairness, but subjective conviction can also reflect bias. Ethical administration requires a reasoned relationship between rules, their purpose and constitutional commitments.

### How to apply it

When a rule appears to produce serious injustice, identify lawful discretion, exceptions, appeal or escalation mechanisms. Document the relevant facts and reasons; do not conceal a departure or substitute personal preference for a public standard. Blind compliance can evade responsibility, while unstructured discretion can create favouritism. A defensible decision explains both why the rule matters and how the chosen response addresses the concrete ethical concern within legitimate authority.
