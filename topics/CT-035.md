## CT-035: Communication and interpersonal scenarios

Prelims CSAT (qualifying) · CSAT · Reasoning / decision making

*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Communication and interpersonal scenarios and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Communication and interpersonal scenarios is a reasoning / decision making skill. List only the facts that are given, symbolize the constraints, and draw a small table, diagram or Venn set. A valid conclusion must follow in every arrangement allowed by the statements. For decision questions, identify stakeholders, legality, urgency and the least harmful workable action.

### Timed application

Attempt this before viewing the hint: Two team members interpret a circular differently. Which first action best reduces misunderstanding? (A) accuse (B) restate each reading and consult the circular (C) ignore it (D) guess Give yourself two minutes, choose an answer and write one line explaining why. Then compare with: Suggested answer / check: B. Retry a similar item after three study days and explain why every rejected option fails.

### Worked example

Use the first exercise as the worked checkpoint. Record the chosen method, answer, elapsed time and error category. The goal is a repeatable process, not a lucky option.

### Recall

- - **q:** What is your first move on a Communication and interpersonal scenarios question?
  - **a:** List the given constraints and test only necessary conclusions.

### Common trap

Choosing a familiar-looking option without proving that it follows from the stated information.
