## GS-316: Welfare scheme design, targeting and leakage

Mains GS2 · Governance · Policy implementation and delivery

*Goal:* Identify who receives welfare and where the delivery chain can fail.

### Choose a targeting approach

Universal provision covers a defined population broadly; targeted provision selects people using criteria such as income, deprivation or location. Targeting can concentrate scarce funds but requires reliable identification. Exclusion error leaves an eligible person out; inclusion error admits an ineligible person. Rapid changes in migration, income or household structure can make an accurate list become outdated.

### Follow the benefit to the household

Leakage can occur through diversion, duplicate records, inflated billing or unauthorized charges. Direct transfers may reduce some intermediaries but cannot fix every eligibility error or missing service. Provide assisted enrollment, transparent criteria, periodic updating and appeals. Evaluate adequacy and timeliness as well as leakage: a perfectly recorded payment delivered after the crisis may still fail its purpose.
