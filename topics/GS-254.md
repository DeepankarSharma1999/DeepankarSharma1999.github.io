## GS-254: Storage, cold chain and marketing

Prelims GS1 + Mains GS3 · Economy · Agriculture and food

*Goal:* Explain Storage, cold chain and marketing and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Why marketing failed farmers

Before independence, farmers selling to traders faced faulty weights and manipulated accounts. Without price information they accepted low prices, and without storage they could not hold back produce for a better price later. NCERT notes that even today over a tenth of farm output is wasted for want of storage. These failures justify state intervention. The marketing chain is long — collection, grading, storage, processing, transport, packaging — and a gap at any link, especially for perishables, turns into loss for the grower.

### Four state responses

NCERT lists four measures. Regulation of markets created orderly, transparent trading and helped both farmers and consumers, though around 27,000 rural periodic markets still await regulation. Physical infrastructure — roads, railways, warehouses, godowns, cold storages and processing units — remains inadequate. Cooperative marketing helped farmers get fair prices, with Gujarat's milk cooperatives the showcase, but cooperatives suffered from thin membership, weak links between marketing and processing, and poor financial management. Finally, price policy through MSP, FCI buffer stocks and PDS. In 2016 the e-NAM portal linked mandis online.

### Alternative channels and cold chains

Direct sales to consumers raise farmers' incomes, as seen in Apni Mandi, Hadaspar Mandi, Rythu Bazars and Uzhavar Sandies. Fast-food chains and others increasingly contract with farmers, supplying seeds and inputs and buying at pre-decided prices; this can reduce price risk and widen markets, though NCERT asks whether small farmers actually gain. Horticulture shows why cold chains matter: fruits, vegetables and flowers are perishable, and NCERT says expanding horticulture needs electricity, cold storage, marketing linkages, small processing units and technology dissemination.
