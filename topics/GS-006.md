## GS-006: Mauryan Empire: administration and Ashoka

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Ancient India

*Goal:* Explain Mauryan Empire: administration and Ashoka and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Mauryan rule linked an imperial centre with provincial administration. Ashoka's inscriptions communicate dhamma, welfare concerns and moral exhortation; they provide direct evidence of royal communication.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Build the topic around three defensible links. Mauryan power joined a central court to provincial centres and varied local arrangements across a large realm. Ashokan edicts are direct royal communications about dhamma, welfare, administration and moral conduct. Dhamma was an ethical-political policy and should not be equated mechanically with formal conversion doctrine. For objective questions, classify each statement by process, chronology or scope before eliminating it. For a written answer or worked solution, state the governing idea, apply it to a concrete example, and finish with the limitation: An inscription states what authority wished to communicate; it does not alone prove uniform implementation.

### Worked example

Rock Edict XIII links the Kalinga war with remorse and a preference for conquest through dhamma.
