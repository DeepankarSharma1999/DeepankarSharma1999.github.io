## GS-389: Codes of conduct versus codes of ethics

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Thinkers, administration and probity

*Goal:* Explain Codes of conduct versus codes of ethics and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

A code of ethics states guiding values and principles; a code of conduct specifies expected or prohibited behaviour. The first supports judgment in new situations; the second offers clearer operational boundaries. Both need institutions that make compliance possible and violations consequential.

### How to apply it

A gift rule, conflict-disclosure process and procurement restrictions translate values into practical conduct. Yet rules cannot anticipate every case, so training and reasoned advice remain necessary. If leaders routinely disregard standards, a polished code loses credibility. Evaluate awareness, accessibility, enforcement and opportunities to raise concerns. Neither vague aspirations nor an exhaustive checklist alone creates an ethical workplace; culture and incentives determine how written standards operate.
