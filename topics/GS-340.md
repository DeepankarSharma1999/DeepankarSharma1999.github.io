## GS-340: BRICS, SCO, G20 and regional forums

Mains GS2 + Prelims GS1 · International Relations · India and world

*Goal:* Compare international forums by membership purpose and decision-making capacity.

### The same country can use several tables

BRICS developed from BRIC coordination and South Africa’s subsequent entry, and has expanded beyond that historical acronym. The SCO focuses on Eurasian cooperation including security. The G20 addresses international economic coordination and includes the African Union following its 2023 admission. Avoid relying on old membership counts or assuming that all these forums have the same agenda.

### Representation does not ensure enforcement

Forums can coordinate positions, develop institutions and give members opportunities to negotiate. Consensus and divergent national interests can limit action. India may use overlapping forums to advance development, financial reform or security cooperation without accepting a single fixed bloc identity. Compare concrete outputs, implementation and representation rather than judging effectiveness only by summit attendance.

### Worked example

A summit agrees to improve digital inclusion. Progress requires national agencies, financing and measurable implementation after leaders leave. A declaration creates a shared political direction; it is not a completed service.

### Recall

- - **q:** Why may forum membership counts become outdated?
  - **a:** Admissions and institutional changes can alter membership.
- - **q:** Can a summit declaration implement itself?
  - **a:** No. States and institutions must translate commitments into action.

### Common trap

Do not assume membership in one forum implies hostility toward every country outside it.
