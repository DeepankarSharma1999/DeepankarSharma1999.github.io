## GS-265: Major world/Indian biomes

Prelims GS1 + Mains GS3 · Environment · Ecology fundamentals

*Goal:* Explain Major world/Indian biomes and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Indian forest biomes by rainfall

NCERT groups Indian forests by vegetation and climate. Tropical evergreen and semi-evergreen forests need over 200 cm rain and above 22°C; they are layered, with trees reaching 60 m or more, and stay green year-round with species like rosewood, mahogany and ebony. Tropical deciduous or monsoon forests are the most widespread: moist types (teak, sal, shisham) in 100–200 cm zones and dry types (tendu, palas, amaltas) in 70–100 cm zones, shedding leaves in the dry season. Thorn forests, below 50 cm, cover semi-arid Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan and Gujarat with leafless scrub and tussocky grass.

### Montane and littoral formations

In mountains, falling temperature with altitude changes vegetation. In the Himalaya, deciduous foothill forests give way to wet temperate forests at 1,000–2,000 m with oak and chestnut, chir pine at 1,500–1,750 m, deodar in the west, blue pine and spruce at 2,225–3,048 m, then silver fir, juniper, birch and rhododendron at 3,000–4,000 m, alpine pastures and finally mosses and lichens of tundra. Southern mountain forests in the Nilgiris, Anaimalai and Palani hills include temperate sholas. Littoral and swamp forests include mangroves along salt marshes, tidal creeks and estuaries, covering 4,992 sq km.

### Why tropical biomes are richest

The latitudinal gradient is the best-known diversity pattern: with few exceptions, tropical latitudes between 23.5°N and 23.5°S hold more species than temperate or polar zones. A tropical forest in Ecuador can have up to ten times the vascular plant species of a similar temperate forest. Three hypotheses explain this: tropics escaped frequent glaciations and had more evolutionary time; their environment is less seasonal and more predictable, encouraging niche specialisation; and greater solar energy raises productivity. India, largely tropical, has over 1,200 bird species. The Amazon rainforest has the greatest diversity on Earth.
