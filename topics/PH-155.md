## PH-155: Hydrogen atom, H2 and H2+ in astronomy

Physics Paper II · Physics · Atomic and molecular

*Goal:* Understand Hydrogen atom, H2 and H2+ in astronomy, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Hydrogen atom, H2 and H2+ in astronomy belongs to Atomic and molecular. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Identify the angular momenta and coupling scheme before assigning term symbols or selection rules. Separate rotational, vibrational and electronic energy scales. For spectra, connect level differences to frequency and explain intensity through population and transition probability.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive the main physical relation(s) governing hydrogen atom, h2 and h2+ in astronomy. Define every symbol, state approximations and provide a labelled sketch where useful. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: For a diatomic molecule or hydrogen-like atom, calculate/identify the spectral transition governed by hydrogen atom, h2 and h2+ in astronomy and its selection rule.
