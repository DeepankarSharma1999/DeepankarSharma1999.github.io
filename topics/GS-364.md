## GS-364: Ethics in private versus public life

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Ethics and human interface

*Goal:* Explain Ethics in private versus public life and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Private ethics concerns personal relationships and choices; public ethics adds obligations attached to authority, public resources and equal citizenship. Loyalty to family is valuable privately but cannot justify preferential treatment in an official decision.

### How to apply it

Public officials must manage conflicts between personal ties and impersonal duties. Disclosure, recusal, recorded reasons and transparent procedures help protect fair decisions and public confidence. A private action can also affect public responsibilities when it creates dependence or an appearance of bias. The distinction therefore concerns roles and obligations, not a claim that private conduct is ethically irrelevant.
