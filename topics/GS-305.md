## GS-305: Indian S&T institutions and achievements

Prelims GS1 + Mains GS3 · Science · General science / technology

*Goal:* Match major Indian science institutions to their functions and outputs.

### Remember functions before acronyms

ISRO develops space capabilities and applications; DRDO works on defence research and development; CSIR coordinates a network of scientific and industrial research laboratories. ICMR supports biomedical research, while ICAR supports agricultural research and education. These functions may overlap through collaboration, but an exam statement assigning every scientific activity to one organization is usually too broad.

### Connect an achievement to a public use

Space missions demonstrate capabilities such as launch, navigation, remote sensing and planetary exploration. Their policy value includes weather information, disaster support and communications. Agricultural research can produce improved varieties; industrial laboratories can develop processes and materials. Judge an achievement through the chain of research, validation, manufacture and adoption rather than remembering a launch or announcement without its application.

### Worked example

Satellite observations identify a cyclone; meteorological interpretation produces a forecast; state officials arrange evacuation. ISRO supplies part of the information chain, while disaster protection requires other scientific and administrative institutions.

### Recall

- - **q:** Which organization specializes in agricultural research?
  - **a:** ICAR.
- - **q:** Why distinguish a research institution from an implementing agency?
  - **a:** Discovery and widespread service delivery require different capabilities.

### Common trap

Institutions collaborate; avoid assuming that one agency owns every stage of a scientific achievement.
