## GS-023: Sikh traditions and institutions

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Medieval India

*Goal:* Explain Sikh traditions and institutions and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Sikh traditions developed from Guru Nanak's teachings through successive Gurus and community institutions. Guru Gobind Singh established the Khalsa in 1699, giving institutional form to a distinctive collective identity.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Successive Gurus developed scripture, congregational practice and institutions around the community begun by Guru Nanak. The Khalsa of 1699 marked a major institutional development but did not erase the longer history of Sikh teaching and organization. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Do not compress two centuries of Sikh development into a single military reaction.

### Worked example

Langar expresses shared participation and challenges exclusion through a common meal.
