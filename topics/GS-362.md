## GS-362: Ethics: meaning, scope and dimensions

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Ethics and human interface

*Goal:* Explain Ethics: meaning, scope and dimensions and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Ethics examines how actions, institutions and character ought to be evaluated. Consequences, duties, rights, justice and virtues provide distinct lenses. Descriptive claims say what people do; normative claims argue what they should do, and require reasons rather than popularity alone.

### How to apply it

An administrative decision can be legal yet ethically troubling if it exploits a loophole or ignores avoidable harm. Conversely, personal conviction cannot automatically override lawful public duties. Ethical analysis identifies affected interests, relevant principles and defensible options, then explains why one course deserves preference. A moral label without a chain of reasoning does not resolve a conflict between genuine values.
