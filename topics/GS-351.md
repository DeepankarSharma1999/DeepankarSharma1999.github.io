## GS-351: Sendai Framework and community preparedness

Mains GS3 · Disaster Management · Risk management

*Goal:* Explain Sendai Framework and community preparedness and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

The Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction covers 2015–2030. Its priorities include understanding risk, strengthening risk governance, investing in resilience, and improving preparedness and recovery. It shifts attention from reacting to disasters toward reducing the conditions that make hazards damaging.

### How to apply it

Community preparedness translates warnings and plans into practical capability. Local risk maps, accessible shelters, evacuation arrangements and rehearsals identify barriers before a crisis. Participation must include people with disabilities, older residents and others who may need assistance. A plan's value depends on usable roles, trusted information and repeated practice, not its length or official signature.
