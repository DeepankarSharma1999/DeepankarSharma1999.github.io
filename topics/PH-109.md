## PH-109: Otto and Diesel engines

Physics Paper I · Physics · Thermodynamics

*Goal:* Understand Otto and Diesel engines, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Otto and Diesel engines belongs to Thermodynamics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Define the system, path and sign convention. Use dU=δQ−δW while remembering that heat and work are path quantities. Entropy, free energy and Maxwell relations require the relevant constraints; check reversible limits before applying ideal-gas shortcuts.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive thermal efficiency of Otto and Diesel cycles and compare assumptions. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: For Otto compression ratio r=8 and γ=1.4, compute η=1−1/r^(γ−1).
