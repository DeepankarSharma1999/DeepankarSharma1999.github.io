## GS-019: Mansabdari, jagirdari and agrarian relations

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Medieval India

*Goal:* Explain Mansabdari, jagirdari and agrarian relations and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Mansab specified a noble's rank and service expectations; jagir assigned revenue claims for remuneration. A jagirdar was distinct from a zamindar with local rights and influence.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. The mansab fixed status and service obligations; a jagir assigned revenue for pay and was normally transferable. Zamindars possessed local social and revenue claims, so they cannot be reduced to salaried Mughal officers. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Mansab, jagir and zamindari describe different relationships and are not interchangeable land titles.

### Worked example

A noble could hold a mansab while receiving revenue from a transferable jagir.
