## GS-063: American and French revolutions

Mains GS1 · World History · 18th–20th century

*Goal:* Explain American and French revolutions and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

The American Revolution challenged imperial rule; the French Revolution transformed monarchy, privilege and citizenship through successive phases. Their declarations coexisted with exclusions from political equality.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. The American struggle challenged imperial rule through a republic, while the French Revolution attacked legal privilege and monarchical sovereignty. Both circulated rights-based political language, yet women, enslaved people and other groups remained excluded or contested. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Shared ideas of rights do not make the two revolutions identical in cause, social structure or sequence.

### Worked example

Compare universal language of rights with the actual position of women and enslaved people.
