## GS-017: Vijayanagara and Bahmani polities

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Medieval India

*Goal:* Explain Vijayanagara and Bahmani polities and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Vijayanagara and Bahmani states organized resources through regional elites, military competition and agrarian networks. Their relations included conflict, negotiation and shared cultural practices.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Vijayanagara rule relied on fortified centres, temple-linked agrarian expansion and nayaka military chiefs. Bahmani-Vijayanagara rivalry over strategic zones coexisted with movement of artisans, styles and political practices across the Deccan. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Conflict between two states does not prove two sealed religious civilizations.

### Worked example

Competition over the Raichur doab had strategic and resource dimensions.
