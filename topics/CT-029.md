## CT-029: Venn diagrams and set logic

Prelims CSAT (qualifying) · CSAT · Reasoning / decision making

*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Venn diagrams and set logic and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Venn diagrams and set logic is a reasoning / decision making skill. List only the facts that are given, symbolize the constraints, and draw a small table, diagram or Venn set. A valid conclusion must follow in every arrangement allowed by the statements. For decision questions, identify stakeholders, legality, urgency and the least harmful workable action.

### Timed application

Attempt this before viewing the hint: Of 50 students, 30 learn Hindi, 25 learn English, 10 learn neither. How many learn both?
