## GS-336: India–Russia cooperation

Mains GS2 + Prelims GS1 · International Relations · India and world

*Goal:* Place India–Russia cooperation within historical continuity and changing constraints.

### Know the historical distinctions

The 1971 treaty involved India and the Soviet Union. India and the Russian Federation established later frameworks, including a strategic partnership in 2000 and an elevated partnership in 2010. Defence equipment, nuclear energy and space cooperation illustrate long-standing connections. Historical goodwill matters, but it does not by itself resolve today’s commercial or strategic difficulties.

### Assess dependence and diversification

Equipment relationships create needs for spares, maintenance, training and upgrades over decades. Energy cooperation can improve supply options while exposing firms to price, transport and financial risks. India must weigh these interests alongside relations with other partners. Diversification seeks resilience, while cooperation can continue where mutually useful and legally feasible.
