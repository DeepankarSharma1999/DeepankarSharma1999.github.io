## GS-054: Integration of princely states

Mains GS1 · History · Post-1947 India

*Goal:* Explain Integration of princely states and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Princely-state integration used accession, negotiation and, in some cases, force. Accession on specified subjects and later administrative merger were related but distinct processes.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Most princely states acceded through negotiation on defence, external affairs and communications before deeper administrative merger. Junagadh, Hyderabad and Kashmir followed different political and military sequences and must be analysed separately. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Accession to India and later integration into a state administration were distinct legal-political steps.

### Worked example

A state's accession did not by itself settle every question of internal political reorganization.
