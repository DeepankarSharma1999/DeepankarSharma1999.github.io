## GS-020: Mughal architecture, art and painting

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Medieval India

*Goal:* Explain Mughal architecture, art and painting and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Mughal artistic traditions combined Persianate, Central Asian and Indian influences. Court painting developed manuscript illustration and portraiture; architecture used varying materials and forms across reigns.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Mughal ateliers organized collaborative manuscript painting, portraiture and close observation under imperial patronage. Architecture changed across reigns through choices of material, garden design, dome, arch and decorative programme. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: A shared Persianate influence does not make every Mughal monument or painting stylistically identical.
