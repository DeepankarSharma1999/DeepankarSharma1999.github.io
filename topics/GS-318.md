## GS-318: Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes protection

Mains GS2 · Social Justice · Rights, welfare and services

*Goal:* Connect protections for SCs and STs to the barriers they address.

### Equality needs protection against exclusion

Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes are constitutionally recognized categories, not interchangeable descriptions of poverty. Article 17 abolishes untouchability. Article 46 directs the state to promote the educational and economic interests of weaker sections, especially SCs and STs. Protective law, representation and development measures address different forms of historical and continuing disadvantage.

### Design for distinct circumstances

The Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act addresses specified offences and related safeguards. Tribal protection also involves land, forest access, culture and participation in decisions affecting communities. Scholarships alone cannot remedy intimidation, displacement or discrimination. Effective implementation needs accessible complaints, timely investigation, legal support and monitoring of outcomes rather than spending totals alone.
