## GS-008: Sangam age and southern dynasties

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Ancient India

*Goal:* Explain Sangam age and southern dynasties and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Sangam poetry illuminates early Tamil landscapes, chiefs, warfare and exchange. Chera, Chola and Pandya traditions appear alongside merchants and varied livelihood zones.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Build the topic around three defensible links. Sangam compositions describe differentiated ecological zones, chiefs, warfare, gifts, merchants and livelihoods. Chera, Chola and Pandya traditions were embedded in exchange across ports and inland production zones. Poetry is valuable historical evidence but uses conventions and praise that require corroboration. For objective questions, classify each statement by process, chronology or scope before eliminating it. For a written answer or worked solution, state the governing idea, apply it to a concrete example, and finish with the limitation: Do not treat every literary description as a dated administrative record or merge early Cholas with the later imperial Cholas.

### Worked example

A poem about a port can be compared with archaeological finds to reconstruct exchange.
