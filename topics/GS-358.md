## GS-358: Money laundering and countermeasures

Mains GS3 + Prelims GS1 · Security · Internal security

*Goal:* Explain Money laundering and countermeasures and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Money laundering disguises the criminal origin or ownership of proceeds. Placement, layering and integration are useful analytical stages, although real transactions need not follow a neat sequence. The objective is to make illicit value appear legitimate or obscure its trail.

### How to apply it

Prevention uses customer due diligence, beneficial-ownership information, transaction monitoring and reporting under applicable law. An unusual transaction is a reason for scrutiny, not automatic proof of crime. Investigators must connect funds, actors and predicate wrongdoing through evidence. Cross-border cooperation matters because companies, accounts and transfers may span jurisdictions; excessive paperwork alone does not ensure effective detection.

### Worked example

Multiple transfers through related entities can obscure the beneficial owner without changing who ultimately controls the money.

### Recall

- - **q:** Why is a suspicious transaction not itself proof of laundering?
  - **a:** Suspicion triggers examination; establishing laundering requires evidence meeting the relevant legal elements.

### Common trap

Equating high transaction volume automatically with criminal proceeds.
