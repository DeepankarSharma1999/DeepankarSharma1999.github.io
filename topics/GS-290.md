## GS-290: Sustainable Development Goals

Prelims GS1 + Mains GS3 · Environment · Climate and treaties

*Goal:* Explain Sustainable Development Goals and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Evolution of the index

The first SDG India Index in 2018 covered 13 goals and 39 targets with 62 indicators, limited by data gaps, yet it started policy dialogue on the SDGs. The 2019-20 edition used 100 indicators and 54 targets across all goals, with Goal 17 handled qualitatively. The 2020-21 edition, themed on partnerships in the Decade of Action, used 115 indicators and 70 targets. The 2023-24 edition covers 16 goals, 70 targets and 113 indicators after three years of consultation with States, UTs and ministries. Throughout, NITI treats the 17 goals as an integral and indivisible 2030 Agenda, with Goal 17 (partnerships) assessed only qualitatively. Because the indicator set keeps changing, the report warns that editions are not strictly comparable.

### How scores are built

Indicators were chosen for relevance to global targets, alignment with MoSPI's National Indicator Framework, official data covering at least 80% of States/UTs and ministry consent; 100 of the 113 are directly aligned with NIF 2024. A 2030 target is set for each indicator: UN targets for 80 indicators, such as a maternal mortality ratio of 70 per 100,000 live births, and Government of India targets for 24. Raw values are rescaled to 0-100, where 100 means the target is met. Goal scores are arithmetic means of indicator scores with equal weights, and the composite is an equal-weight mean of goal scores, with Goal 14 left out because it mainly concerns coastal States/UTs.

### Results and localisation

States and UTs are grouped as Achievers at 100, Front Runners at 65-99, Performers at 50-64 and Aspirants below 50. In 2023-24, no State scored 100 on all goals; 24 of 28 States were Front Runners and 4 were Performers, while all 8 UTs were Front Runners. India's overall composite score went up to 71 in 2023-24, compared with 66 in 2020-21. NITI has pushed localisation further: a North Eastern Region District SDG Index compares 129 districts on 84 indicators, and an SDG Urban Index ranks 56 cities on 77 indicators. Only 16 of the 113 indicators are scheme-based, as NITI shifts towards outcome measures.
