## PH-185: Bragg law and X-ray diffraction

Physics Paper II · Physics · Solid state physics

*Goal:* Understand Bragg law and X-ray diffraction, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Bragg law and X-ray diffraction belongs to Solid state physics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Connect microscopic order to the measurement: lattice plus basis gives structure, reciprocal spacing controls diffraction, and electronic states form bands. State the approximation and temperature regime. Distinguish a material property from the instrument used to infer it.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive Bragg condition using path difference of waves from crystal planes. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: For λ=0.15 nm and d=0.30 nm, find first-order Bragg angle.
