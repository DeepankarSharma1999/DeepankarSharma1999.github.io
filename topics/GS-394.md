## GS-394: Ethical case study: options and consequences

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Thinkers, administration and probity

*Goal:* Explain Ethical case study: options and consequences and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Options analysis compares feasible courses of action rather than inventing one obvious answer. Assess legality, rights, fairness, consequences, institutional trust and implementation capacity. Include the effects of doing nothing, because inaction is itself a choice with consequences.

### How to apply it

For each option, identify benefits, likely harms, affected groups and safeguards. Distinguish immediate effects from longer-term incentives, and test how uncertainty might change the ranking. Alternatives can sometimes combine principles, such as limited disclosure with privacy protection. Avoid a false binary when staged action, consultation or review is possible. The purpose is a transparent judgment, not numerical scoring that disguises unsupported assumptions.
