## GS-057: Land reform and planned development

Mains GS1 · History · Post-1947 India

*Goal:* Explain Land reform and planned development and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Post-independence land reform addressed intermediaries, tenancy and land ceilings; implementation varied by state. Planning directed public investment toward development priorities under resource constraints.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Land reform included abolition of intermediaries, tenancy regulation, ceilings and consolidation, with implementation varying sharply across states. Five-Year Plans combined public investment and a mixed economy with debates over agriculture, industry and redistribution. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Abolishing zamindari did not by itself complete redistribution or secure every tenant.
