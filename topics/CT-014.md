## CT-014: Simple and compound interest

Prelims CSAT (qualifying) · CSAT · Quantitative aptitude

*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Simple and compound interest and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Simple and compound interest is a quantitative aptitude skill. Translate words into variables, write the relation before calculating, and estimate the answer range. Keep fractions until the last step when possible. If options are available, test them only after understanding the relation; record whether an error came from concept, setup, arithmetic or time pressure.

### Timed application

Attempt this before viewing the hint: On ₹1000 at 10% annual rate for two years, compare simple and compound interest. Give yourself two minutes, choose an answer and write one line explaining why. Then compare with: Suggested answer / check: SI ₹200; CI ₹210; difference ₹10. Retry a similar item after three study days and explain why every rejected option fails.

### Worked example

Use the first exercise as the worked checkpoint. Record the chosen method, answer, elapsed time and error category. The goal is a repeatable process, not a lucky option.

### Recall

- - **q:** What is your first move on a Simple and compound interest question?
  - **a:** Define variables and write the relation before calculating.

### Common trap

Choosing a familiar-looking option without proving that it follows from the stated information.
