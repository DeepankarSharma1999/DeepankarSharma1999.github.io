## GS-007: Post-Mauryan kingdoms and trade

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Ancient India

*Goal:* Explain Post-Mauryan kingdoms and trade and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

After Mauryan decline, regional powers and Indo-Greek, Shaka and Kushana rulers shaped political networks. Trade, coinage and patronage connected Indian regions with Central Asia and the Mediterranean.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Build the topic around three defensible links. Post-Mauryan India contained overlapping regional powers including Indo-Greek, Shaka, Satavahana and Kushana rulers. Coins, ports, caravan routes and craft centres show exchange linking the subcontinent with Central Asia and the Roman world. Political fragmentation can coexist with commercial and cultural integration. For objective questions, classify each statement by process, chronology or scope before eliminating it. For a written answer or worked solution, state the governing idea, apply it to a concrete example, and finish with the limitation: Do not describe the entire period between Mauryas and Guptas as a political or economic vacuum.
