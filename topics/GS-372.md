## GS-372: Moral and political attitudes

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Attitude, aptitude and emotional intelligence

*Goal:* Explain Moral and political attitudes and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Moral attitudes evaluate conduct as right or wrong; political attitudes concern authority, policy and collective life. They are shaped by socialization, interests, identity and reflection. Strong conviction does not itself establish a claim's factual accuracy or ethical validity.

### How to apply it

Public officials may hold political opinions privately while applying law impartially. Distinguish disagreement over policy from discriminatory treatment of citizens. Moral attitudes also vary in their reasoning: a judgment may rest on consequences, duties or group loyalty. Fair administration requires examining evidence and constitutional commitments, including the interests of people with whom the official disagrees, rather than translating personal preference into unequal public service.
