## GS-348: Landslides, heat waves and urban floods

Mains GS3 · Disaster Management · Risk management

*Goal:* Explain how urban and land-use choices amplify landslide, heat and flood risk.

### Risk grows through local conditions

Slope instability can increase with weak geology, heavy rain, drainage changes and unsafe cutting. Heat exposure rises where shade is scarce, work is strenuous or housing retains heat. Urban flooding can worsen when drains are blocked, wetlands are lost and impermeable surfaces accelerate runoff. These hazards have different mechanisms, even when an intense weather event affects them together.

### Choose complementary measures

Slope management needs drainage, land-use control and engineering suited to local evidence. Heat action links forecasts, communication, water access, cooler spaces and occupational safeguards. Urban flood reduction combines maintained drainage, retention areas, protected waterways and planning. Evaluate impacts on low-income residents: relocation or infrastructure can create new vulnerability if livelihoods and access are ignored.
