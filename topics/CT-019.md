## CT-019: Algebraic equations and inequalities

Prelims CSAT (qualifying) · CSAT · Quantitative aptitude

*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Algebraic equations and inequalities and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Algebraic equations and inequalities is a quantitative aptitude skill. Translate words into variables, write the relation before calculating, and estimate the answer range. Keep fractions until the last step when possible. If options are available, test them only after understanding the relation; record whether an error came from concept, setup, arithmetic or time pressure.

### Timed application

Attempt this before viewing the hint: Solve: 3x − 7 ≤ 11. Give yourself two minutes, choose an answer and write one line explaining why. Then compare with: Suggested answer / check: x ≤ 6. Retry a similar item after three study days and explain why every rejected option fails.
