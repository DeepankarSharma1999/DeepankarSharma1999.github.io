## GS-345: Disaster risk assessment and mapping

Mains GS3 · Disaster Management · Risk management

*Goal:* Build a risk map that connects hazard data to decisions.

### Layer information rather than only drawing danger zones

Risk assessment combines the likelihood and severity of a hazard with exposed people, buildings, infrastructure and vulnerabilities. A flood map may show water depth, but evacuation planning also needs settlement locations, road access and people needing assistance. Historical data, terrain, satellite observations and local knowledge contribute different evidence, each with limitations.

### Use uncertainty honestly

A map depends on assumptions about rainfall, land use, drainage and building strength. Updating matters because development can change runoff or increase exposed assets. Present plausible scenarios and identify critical facilities whose failure has wider consequences. Assess who might be missed by official records, including informal settlements. The purpose is to guide safer location, investment and preparedness, not simply produce an attractive map.
