## GS-388: Transparency and information sharing

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Thinkers, administration and probity

*Goal:* Explain Transparency and information sharing and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Information sharing enables scrutiny of public decisions and helps citizens exercise entitlements. Transparency includes understandable reasons, accessible records and timely publication, not merely dumping large datasets. Privacy, security and legitimate confidentiality require reasoned protection rather than blanket secrecy.

### How to apply it

Disclosure design matters. Publish relevant criteria, spending and outcomes while minimizing unnecessary personal details. A useful record should explain who decided, under what authority and on what evidence. Where information is withheld, apply the governing legal test and available review mechanisms. The ethical objective is informed accountability alongside rights, so neither maximum disclosure nor maximum secrecy is a sufficient principle in every case.
