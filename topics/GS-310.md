## GS-310: Citizens Charters and service delivery

Mains GS2 · Governance · Policy implementation and delivery

*Goal:* Design a Citizens’ Charter that a service user can actually use.

### Make a service promise specific

A Citizens’ Charter states an organization’s service commitments, standards and grievance channels. It should tell a person what service is offered, required documents, expected time, responsible office and route for escalation. “We provide excellent service” cannot be tested. “A complete application receives a decision within a stated period” creates a measurable expectation.

### Close the delivery gap

Consult users, simplify procedures and give staff the resources needed to meet commitments. Display standards in local languages and accessible formats. Track delays and publish performance rather than quietly changing deadlines. A charter by itself is not automatically a legally enforceable service guarantee; enforceability depends on applicable law and institutional arrangements. Feedback should lead to process correction.
