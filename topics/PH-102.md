## PH-102: Planck radiation law

Physics Paper I · Physics · EM waves and radiation

*Goal:* Understand Planck radiation law, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Planck radiation law belongs to EM waves and radiation. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Start from Maxwell’s equations and the material relations. For a plane wave verify E, B and propagation direction, then use the Poynting vector for energy flow. At an interface apply tangential boundary conditions; for radiation laws state the variable and integration range.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive Planck spectral radiance and identify its classical limits. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: Why does Rayleigh–Jeans approximation fail at short wavelengths?

### Worked example

Worked route: identify the requested result in “Derive Planck spectral radiance and identify its classical limits.”; select the governing principle; state assumptions; carry the algebra without skipping vector directions or boundary conditions; then interpret the result physically. Use the supplied self-check rubric: Assessment: principle and diagram → algebra/derivation → assumptions → units/limiting case.
