## GS-269: Protected areas: national parks sanctuaries reserves

Prelims GS1 + Mains GS3 · Environment · Ecology fundamentals

*Goal:* Explain Protected areas: national parks sanctuaries reserves and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Legal categories under the Wildlife Act

The Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972, in its text as on 5 June 2025, sets up graded categories. A state may notify a sanctuary over an area it judges ecologically, faunally or florally significant; its Chief Wild Life Warden may regulate, control or even prohibit grazing by livestock. A national park, under section 35, is stricter: claims must be settled and land rights vested in the state, no livestock grazing is allowed, removing wildlife or forest produce needs a permit cleared with the National Board for Wild Life, and boundaries change only on that Board's advice. Conservation reserves (section 36A) are government lands, particularly those beside parks and sanctuaries or connecting them, declared after consulting local communities. Community reserves (section 36C) cover private or community land where people volunteer to protect wildlife, managed by a local committee.

### Tiger reserves and Project Tiger

Under section 38V a state notifies a tiger reserve on the advice of the National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA). Each reserve has a core or critical tiger habitat, kept inviolate on scientific grounds without overriding tribal rights, and a buffer with lighter protection that allows dispersal and coexistence with local livelihoods. Boundaries cannot be altered, nor a reserve de-notified, without NTCA and National Board approval. Tigers had fallen to 1,827 by 1973, when Project Tiger began with nine reserves on 16,339 sq km. MoEFCC's 2025-26 report says three new notifications, one in Chhattisgarh and Ratapani and Madhav in Madhya Pradesh, took the total to 58, nearly 2.5 per cent of India's area. Counts rose from 1,411 (2006) to 3,682 (2023).

### Network size, buffers and communities

Counts depend on the source year. The current Class 11 NCERT lists 107 national parks and 573 sanctuaries, while the Class 12 biology book still gives 90 and 448. MoEFCC's 2023-24 report says protected areas grew from 745 in 2014 to 998, about 5.28 per cent of the country, with community reserves up from 43 to 220. Its 2025-26 report counts 688 national parks and sanctuaries, a figure limited to those two categories and so not comparable with the 998 total, and describes Eco-Sensitive Zones as shock absorbers that grade protection outward; 355 final ESZ notifications covered 73,602.71 sq km by 31 December 2025. Local people strengthen protection: five Alwar villages declared 1,200 hectares as the Bhairodev Dakav 'Sonchuri' with their own hunting ban, and Bishnoi villages guard blackbuck, nilgai and peacocks.

### Worked example

Matching drill: grazing regulated → sanctuary (s.18/33); grazing barred, rights vested in state → national park (s.35); government land linking protected areas → conservation reserve (s.36A); volunteered private/community land → community reserve (s.36C); inviolate core + buffer on NTCA advice → tiger reserve (s.38V). Mains frame — 'Has Project Tiger succeeded?': baseline 1,827 (1973), 9 → 58 reserves, 1,411 (2006) → 3,682 (2023), continuing threats and the need for community partnership.

### Recall

- - **q:** How many national parks and sanctuaries does Class 11 NCERT report?
  - **a:** 107 national parks and 573 wildlife sanctuaries.
- - **q:** How many tiger reserves are there now and in how many states?
  - **a:** 58 reserves in 18 states.
- - **q:** What was India's tiger count in 2023 per NCERT?
  - **a:** 3,682.
- - **q:** Under which section is a conservation reserve declared, and on whose land?
  - **a:** Section 36A; government-owned land, especially adjoining or linking protected areas.
- - **q:** What two zones make up a tiger reserve under section 38V?
  - **a:** Core or critical tiger habitat (inviolate) and buffer or peripheral area.

### Common trap

Using outdated counts or mixing categories: the Class 12 biology book still states 90 national parks and 448 sanctuaries, while the updated geography book gives 107 and 573; and grazing may be regulated in a sanctuary but is barred in a national park.
