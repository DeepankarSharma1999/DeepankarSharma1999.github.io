## GS-314: Role of NGOs, SHGs and civil society

Mains GS2 · Governance · Policy implementation and delivery

*Goal:* Distinguish how NGOs, SHGs and wider civil society contribute to development.

### Different forms of collective action

A non-governmental organization can provide services, conduct research or advocate rights. A self-help group usually organizes members around mutual support, savings and credit, and may develop collective livelihoods. Civil society is broader: associations, community organizations and citizen movements create spaces between the household, market and state. These forms overlap but are not interchangeable institutions.

### Partnership needs accountability

Local knowledge can reveal excluded households and improve programme design. Collective action may strengthen bargaining power and women’s public participation. Risks include elite capture, opaque finance, weak representation and dependence on a single donor. Public partnerships should define responsibilities, publish relevant information and include independent feedback. Government retains responsibility for rights and essential services even when a voluntary organization helps deliver them.
