## GS-058: Green Revolution and social changes

Mains GS1 · History · Post-1947 India

*Goal:* Explain Green Revolution and social changes and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

The Green Revolution combined high-yielding varieties with irrigation, fertilizers and institutional support. Output gains were significant but concentrated by crop, region and access to inputs.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Higher-yield seeds required reliable water, fertilizer, credit, procurement and extension rather than seed alone. Early gains concentrated in selected crops and regions and changed rural class, labour and ecological relations. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Do not attribute production growth to one input or assume equal benefits across farmers and regions.
