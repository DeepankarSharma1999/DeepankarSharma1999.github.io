## PH-143: Spin half and Pauli matrices

Physics Paper II · Physics · Quantum mechanics

*Goal:* Understand Spin half and Pauli matrices, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Spin half and Pauli matrices belongs to Quantum mechanics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Write the state, operator and boundary conditions. Normalize before calculating probabilities or expectation values. Eigenvalues are measurement outcomes; a general state is a superposition. Check Hermiticity, units and limiting behaviour rather than treating a memorized wavefunction as universal.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Show Pauli spin matrices, eigenvalues, commutators and spin measurement probabilities. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: For spin-up in z direction, find probability of measuring spin-up along x.
