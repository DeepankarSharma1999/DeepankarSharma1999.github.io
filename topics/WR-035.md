## WR-035: Short essay: coherent structure

English Paper B (qualifying) · Writing/Language · Reading and writing

*Goal:* Practise Short essay: coherent structure with a clear structure, a strict word limit and a short revision pass.

### Technique

Read the instruction and word limit before drafting. Preserve the author’s meaning, organize one idea per sentence, and prefer precise plain English. In comprehension, separate what the passage states from what it permits you to infer. In writing, revise agreement, tense, articles, prepositions and punctuation. For “Short essay: coherent structure”, write a tiny plan first: task, central idea, supporting points and final check. This reduces working-memory load and makes it easier to resume after an interruption.

### Apply now

Complete this exercise: Write a 120–150-word response illustrating short essay: coherent structure. Check grammar, clarity and word economy. Use the self-check only after drafting: Self-check: exact meaning, correct grammar, adequate structure and strict word limit. Then perform a second pass for clarity and format. If the task is a full paper or PYQ set, divide it into sections and record time used rather than trying to review an entire PDF at once.

### Worked example

A strong response follows four visible moves: interpret the instruction, outline the answer, draft within the limit, and revise against the stated check. For transfer practice, use: Find one compulsory English PYQ testing “Short essay: coherent structure” and complete it under time pressure.

### Recall

- - **q:** What four checks should you make for Short essay: coherent structure?
  - **a:** Instruction, central meaning, structure and language accuracy; also verify the required word limit or time limit.

### Common trap

Writing everything known about the theme instead of completing the precise language or argument task.
