## GS-301: Cybersecurity fundamentals and networks

Prelims GS1 + Mains GS3 · Science · General science / technology

*Goal:* Trace a network attack and choose a defence at each weak point.

### Protect three properties

Confidentiality restricts access to information, integrity prevents unauthorized alteration and availability keeps services usable. A network links devices through agreed protocols; an address identifies a destination while encryption protects content in transit. A firewall filters traffic, but it cannot by itself stop an authorized employee from entering credentials into a convincing fake website.

### Build overlapping defences

Phishing manipulates trust; malware executes unwanted code; ransomware can encrypt files and demand payment. Reduce exposure through timely updates, least-privilege access, multifactor authentication and tested offline backups. Detection logs and an incident-response process matter because prevention can fail. Isolate an affected device, preserve evidence and use authorized reporting channels rather than deleting everything immediately.
