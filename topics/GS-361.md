## GS-361: Security forces, agencies and mandates

Mains GS3 + Prelims GS1 · Security · Internal security

*Goal:* Explain Security forces, agencies and mandates and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Security institutions have distinct mandates. Border forces guard assigned frontiers; the CRPF supports internal-security and law-and-order tasks; the CISF protects specified installations and facilities. State police retain central roles in ordinary policing, while specialized agencies address defined national responsibilities.

### How to apply it

Compare each institution through its legal basis, jurisdiction, command and operational role. Intelligence collection, investigation, prosecution and adjudication are distinct functions: information may guide an inquiry without constituting courtroom proof. Interagency coordination matters where threats cross sectors or borders, but coordination does not erase statutory limits. Check current official mandates before assigning a specific case to an agency.

### Worked example

A facility-protection requirement and a criminal investigation may involve different institutions working within separate powers.

### Recall

- - **q:** Why distinguish intelligence from investigation and adjudication?
  - **a:** They serve different purposes and evidentiary standards; collecting information does not establish legal guilt.

### Common trap

Assuming one national agency has unlimited authority over every security matter.
