## GS-360: Social media, misinformation and security

Mains GS3 + Prelims GS1 · Security · Internal security

*Goal:* Explain Social media, misinformation and security and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Social media enables rapid communication but can also amplify misinformation, impersonation and coordinated manipulation. Misinformation concerns false content regardless of intent; disinformation involves deliberate deception. Virality measures spread, not accuracy, and repeated exposure can create unwarranted confidence.

### How to apply it

Security responses should examine the specific harm, evidence and proportionality of intervention. Timely authoritative information, media literacy and platform accountability can complement lawful enforcement. Broad restrictions may impose costs on legitimate communication and emergency coordination. Distinguish criticism, error and incitement rather than treating all unwelcome content alike; credible corrections address the claim and its evidence instead of attacking an entire group.
