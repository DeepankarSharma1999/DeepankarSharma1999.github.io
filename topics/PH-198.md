## PH-198: Operational amplifiers

Physics Paper II · Physics · Devices and electronics

*Goal:* Understand Operational amplifiers, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Operational amplifiers belongs to Devices and electronics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Start from the device characteristic and operating region, then build the circuit relation. Mark bias polarity, current direction and reference ground. For logic, write the truth table before simplifying; for amplifiers distinguish voltage gain, power gain, feedback and saturation.

### How to practise it

Use this exact checkpoint: Derive inverting op-amp gain assuming ideal negative feedback. Work line by line: list the givens or starting law, draw the geometry where useful, derive symbolically, then substitute numbers. End with units, sign, scale and a limiting-case check. After that attempt the second application without notes: For Rin=10 kΩ and Rf=100 kΩ, find output for +0.2 V input.
