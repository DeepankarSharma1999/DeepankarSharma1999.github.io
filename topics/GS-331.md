## GS-331: India–Bangladesh ties

Mains GS2 + Prelims GS1 · International Relations · India and world

*Goal:* Explain why geography connects India–Bangladesh cooperation and disputes.

### Borders can connect as well as divide

India and Bangladesh share a long land boundary and many transboundary rivers. Geography links trade, water management, transport and security to the lives of border communities. The Land Boundary Agreement implementation in 2015 addressed enclaves and related boundary problems. This shows how a negotiated territorial solution can improve documentation, public services and everyday administrative certainty.

### Identify gains and distributional concerns

Connectivity through roads, rail, waterways and ports can reduce transport costs, including for India’s Northeast. Electricity trade and cross-border commerce can create mutual gains. However, river flows, migration concerns, border incidents and unequal perceptions of benefit require patient negotiation. Assess who gains, who bears costs and which institutions can manage recurring disputes.
