## GS-002: Indus Valley chronology, sites and urbanism

Prelims GS I (Mains culture cross-links) · History · Ancient India

*Goal:* Explain Indus Valley chronology, sites and urbanism and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

Mature Harappan cities flourished approximately 2600–1900 BCE. Planned streets, drainage, seals and standardized weights indicate coordinated urban life; the script remains undeciphered.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Build the topic around three defensible links. The Mature Harappan phase is generally placed around 2600–1900 BCE, within a longer regional sequence. Urban features included planned streets, drainage, craft specialization, long-distance exchange and standardized weights. The script remains undeciphered, so social and political interpretations must rely heavily on material evidence. For objective questions, classify each statement by process, chronology or scope before eliminating it. For a written answer or worked solution, state the governing idea, apply it to a concrete example, and finish with the limitation: Avoid calling every Harappan settlement a city or claiming certainty about rulers, religion or language.

Dholavira's water reservoirs show adaptation to a dry environment.
