## GS-374: Civil service aptitude and values

Mains GS4 · Ethics · Attitude, aptitude and emotional intelligence

*Goal:* Explain Civil service aptitude and values and use it to reason through the worked example.

### Concept and distinctions

Civil-service aptitude concerns capabilities suited to public responsibilities, including analysis, judgment and problem solving. Foundational values guide how those capabilities are used. A technically skilled officer may still fail ethically if competence serves bias or personal advantage.

### How to apply it

Public-service orientation connects expertise with citizens' needs, constitutional commitments and accountability. Recruitment or training cannot measure it through technical scores alone. Assess conduct under pressure, willingness to hear affected people, responsiveness to evidence and ability to explain decisions. Values may conflict in a case, so the officer must integrate fairness, effectiveness and lawful procedure rather than choose one attractive word and ignore the rest.
