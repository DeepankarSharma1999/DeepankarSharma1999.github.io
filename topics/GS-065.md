## GS-065: Nationalism and unification of Germany and Italy

Mains GS1 · World History · 18th–20th century

*Goal:* Explain Nationalism and unification of Germany and Italy and apply the distinction in the worked example.

### Core mechanism

German and Italian unification combined nationalism with diplomacy, war and state leadership. Political unification redrew boundaries without creating culturally homogeneous societies.

### Exam-facing synthesis

Use a three-step frame: identify the institution or event, explain the mechanism, then state its limit. Italian and German unification mixed cultural nationalism with dynastic diplomacy, war and leadership by Piedmont-Sardinia and Prussia. New states unified territories politically while retaining regional, linguistic and social differences. For Prelims, attach each statement to the correct actor, period and legal or social meaning. For Mains, connect cause to mechanism and consequence, then qualify the argument: Popular nationalism mattered, but neither unification was achieved by popular revolution alone.
