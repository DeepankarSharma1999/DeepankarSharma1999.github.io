*Goal:* Build a reliable method for Sequences and number series and apply it under CSAT time pressure.

### Method

Sequences and number series is a quantitative aptitude skill. Translate words into variables, write the relation before calculating, and estimate the answer range. Keep fractions until the last step when possible.
