## PH-142: Hydrogen atom solutions

Physics Paper II · Physics · Quantum mechanics

*Goal:* Understand Hydrogen atom solutions, set up its governing relations, and complete one UPSC-style derivation or application.

### What to learn

Hydrogen atom solutions belongs to Quantum mechanics. Build the topic around definitions, the governing equation, assumptions, and one physical interpretation. Write the state, operator and boundary conditions. Normalize before calculating probabilities or expectation values. Eigenvalues are measurement outcomes; a general state is a superposition. Check Hermiticity, units and limiting behaviour rather than treating a memorized wavefunction as universal.
